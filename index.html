NVIDIA Board Says Jensen Huang Has Resigned; Colette Kress Named Interim CEO

NVIDIA said on November 12 that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, CEO, and a board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress, who previously held finance roles at Cisco and Microsoft, is interim CEO while remaining CFO, and the board is searching for a permanent successor. Harvey C. Jones, former Synopsys CEO, becomes the board's first chair. NVIDIA said operations, customer commitments, and the product roadmap should continue, and it still plans to report fiscal Q3 2027 results on November 17.

Source

F5 Relevance

A leadership change at the center of the AI factory ecosystem can shift partner priorities and alliance timing even if the stated roadmap does not. F5 should watch whether OpenShell, secure-agent, and networking partnerships stay on cadence, and treat the November 17 earnings call as the first public signal under interim leadership.

University of Alabama Builds a Statewide HPC Center on Dell and NVIDIA

Dell Technologies said on November 11 that the University of Alabama is deploying a statewide High Performance Computing Center on Dell PowerEdge servers, NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and H200 NVL GPUs, NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand, and Dell PowerScale storage. The NIST-backed facility is meant as shared HPC and AI infrastructure for the UA System plus other universities, government agencies, and industry partners, covering simulation, machine learning, and large unstructured research datasets.

Source

F5 Relevance

Shared multi-institution AI and HPC traffic in front of PowerScale and InfiniBand GPU clusters is an application-delivery and data-access problem. F5 can position BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud as the secure front door for research apps and S3-style access into this Dell AI data platform.

November 12, 2026

Intel Launches the K-AI Full Stack Alliance in Seoul

Intel Korea launched the K-AI Full Stack Alliance on November 12 at its AI Summit Seoul, with 23 initial members spanning hardware, software, and industry solutions around domestic servers for sovereign AI and physical AI. About 1,000 people from 52 companies attended, including HPE, Dell Technologies, SK Hynix, SK Telecom, Samsung, Naver, and KT. Intel Korea also signed an MoU with the Seoul AI Hub to help startups build and verify solutions.

Source

F5 Relevance

A sovereign and edge AI stack built on domestic servers still needs consistent application delivery, API security, and identity between shared models and industrial endpoints. F5 can join that full-stack story on the traffic and policy layer rather than on silicon.

Nutanix
November 12, 2026

Nutanix Expands NC2 on Azure at Microsoft Ignite

Nutanix said on November 12 that it is expanding Nutanix Cloud Clusters (NC2) on Azure with Microsoft at Ignite 2026. The update adds the AN48 instance type, initially in US East and US West 2, plus an ESAN improvement that drops VWAN to lower total cost as clusters scale. The point is to keep a familiar Nutanix operating model while moving workloads onto Azure BareMetal for migration, disaster recovery, and hybrid placement.

Source

F5 Relevance

Customers dropping VWAN on NC2 still need a secure path for apps between the data center and Azure BareMetal. F5 Distributed Cloud and BIG-IP can cover that interconnect and application delivery without forcing a full network rebuild.
