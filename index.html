Cisco · October 14, 2026

Cisco Warns of Active Attacks on Critical Secure Email Gateway File-Write Flaw

The Hacker News reported October 14 that Cisco is warning of active attacks against CVE-2026-76519 (CVSS 9.8), a path-traversal file-write flaw in Cisco Secure Email Gateway that lets an unauthenticated sender write files to the appliance OS when File Reputation is enabled and content scanner tools are outdated. Cisco shipped hardening releases (AsyncOS 15.5.5-014 and 16.5.0-780, plus Content Scanner Tools 25.3.0.5525+) that install without a restart. ISSSource separately noted October 15 that the same hardening cycle also covers Secure Email and Web Manager, with no workarounds.

Source

F5 Relevance

Email security gateways sit on the same inspection path as F5 BIG-IP and NGINX WAF deployments. An unauthenticated file-write on a widely deployed mail appliance is a concrete reason for joint customers to review east-west policy, TLS inspection placement, and whether F5 can front or segment management planes while Cisco patches land.

Epic · October 15, 2026

Epic Announces Prescription Marketplace for Pharmacy Cash-Price Comparison

Epic said October 15 that Prescription Marketplace will let clinicians and patients at Epic health systems compare a medication's insurance price and pharmacy cash price before the prescription is sent. Any pharmacy can share cash prices with Epic customers. Amazon Pharmacy, Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drugs, Costco, and LillyDirect are among early participants. Epic says Real-Time Prescription Benefit already helped patients save more than $250 million in 2025; the new marketplace is planned for summer 2027. Reported by Epic via PR Newswire; Modern Healthcare also covered it the same day.

Source

F5 Relevance

A point-of-care price API that calls many pharmacies is a high-volume, identity-sensitive integration surface. F5 can sit in front of those pharmacy and payer endpoints with API gateway, WAF, and mTLS so health systems get consistent auth, rate limits, and audit on traffic that leaves the EHR.

HPE GreenLake · October 14, 2026

HPE Will Not Merge Aruba Central and Mist, but Will Share AI, Apps, and Hardware

Fierce Network reported October 14 that HPE networking president Rami Rahim told analysts Aruba Central and Juniper Mist will stay separate platforms — Mist for cloud, Aruba Central for on-premises — rather than collapsing into one management plane. The platforms already share the Marvis AI engine, and new dual-platform Wi-Fi 7 access points and CX switches are meant to run on either. HPE gave no date for full convergence and framed the approach as a way to compete with Cisco in campus networking.

Source

F5 Relevance

A dual-control-plane campus strategy means joint customers will keep two management domains and a shared AI engine. That is a practical opening for F5 to standardize ingress, identity-aware access, and WAF policy across Aruba and Mist sites instead of waiting for a single HPE console.

Nvidia · October 15, 2026

Adobe and NVIDIA Expand Collaboration to Speed Agentic Creation

NVIDIA and Adobe announced October 15 that they are expanding their partnership to optimize Photoshop, Premiere, Substance 3D, Frame.io, and Brand Concierge. Adobe is bringing the NVIDIA RTX Spark superchip into those agentic creative apps, claiming up to 2x faster AI processing, editing, color, and effects. Brand Concierge is now optimized with NVIDIA Nemotron open models, and NVIDIA will use Brand Concierge for contextual interactions on NVIDIA.com. Enterprise availability of the Nemotron-optimized Brand Concierge is planned later this year.

Source

F5 Relevance

Agentic creative apps that call Nemotron models and RTX Spark locally still need a governed path to enterprise data and model endpoints. F5 can attach API gateway, WAF, and AI traffic controls on that path so joint customers get identity, rate limits, and prompt inspection without sitting inside the GPU.
