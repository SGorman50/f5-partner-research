NetAppOctober 26, 2026

NetApp and Emma Technologies Bridge On-Prem ONTAP to European Neocloud GPUs

SDxCentral reported October 26 from GTC Berlin that NetApp has a strategic collaboration with Luxembourg-based Emma Technologies so enterprises can extend data already on ONTAP to rented neocloud GPUs without copying it. NetApp EMEA CTO Jurgen Hofkens described the arrangement as a product use, a paying customer, and a channel opportunity, and said customers retain control of the data because they buy software rather than handing custody to a cloud operator. ChannelPro and ITdaily also covered the pairing as a sovereign AI stack for European businesses.

Source: SDxCentral

F5 Relevance

Extending ONTAP to rented GPUs without a data copy creates a new east-west and north-south path between enterprise storage and neocloud inference. F5 can sit on that path as the secure delivery and API gateway layer: TLS termination, identity-aware access, and WAF/DDoS controls for the S3 and NFS flows that feed GPU tenants, without forcing a second copy of the data.

NVIDIA Is Reportedly in Talks to Back Nscale’s Anthropic Data-Center Financing

Reuters, citing The Information, reported October 29–30 that NVIDIA is in talks to provide AI cloud provider Nscale with billions of dollars in credit guarantees. The support would back debt funding for power and construction of a West Virginia data center earmarked for Anthropic. No agreement is signed and terms could change. Anthropic has a reported plan to spend up to about $45 billion renting compute from that campus, and Nscale is separately discussing selling about 460 MW of AI compute to Anthropic. NVIDIA, Nscale, and Anthropic had not commented as of the Reuters report. This is reported talks, not a confirmed deal.

Source: Reuters via The Economic Times

F5 Relevance

A multi-gigawatt Anthropic campus financed with NVIDIA credit support is another large inference and agent estate that will need governed ingress, not just GPUs. F5 can propose AI traffic management, API gateway, and WAF controls for the tool-calling and model-serving paths into that cluster, alongside Spectrum-X rather than instead of it. Label the financing as reported until NVIDIA or Nscale confirms it.
