City of Cape Town Saves 1,192 Staff Hours with Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform

Red Hat said on November 16 that the City of Cape Town is using Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform to automate patching and day-to-day administration across about 300 SAP systems. After a 14-day proof of concept and a 10-day implementation with Red Hat Consulting, the city reports 1,192 staff hours saved, shorter maintenance windows, and less downtime risk on resident-facing digital channels and emergency systems. The estate also runs Red Hat Enterprise Linux (including for SAP) and Red Hat Satellite, and the city is looking at Event-Driven Ansible and IT service management integration.

F5 Relevance

A large public-sector automation win shows Ansible as the control plane for always-on application estates. F5 can pair BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud with those playbooks so WAF, TLS, and application-delivery changes follow the same repeatable path as the rest of the infrastructure.

DellNovember 15, 2026

DataRobot and Dell Bring Sovereign Agentic AI to Middle East Enterprises

DataRobot said it is collaborating with Dell Technologies to help Middle East enterprises run agentic AI while keeping control of where agents run, how they are governed, and what they cost. The stack combines the Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA and DataRobot’s Agent Workforce Platform for sovereign, on-premises, private, and hybrid-cloud deployments, and was shown at Dell Technologies Forum Dubai, where DataRobot was an AI sponsor. DataRobot CEO Debanjan Saha framed the work as infrastructure-aware agentic AI that does not require handing sensitive data to a public cloud. ZAWYA carried the release dated November 15, 2026.

F5 Relevance

A Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA plus a governed agent platform is a concrete sovereign-AI deployment pattern. F5 can sit in front of that factory as the secure ingress and policy point for agent and inference APIs, covering TLS, identity-aware access, and inspection without moving the data plane off the customer’s infrastructure.
