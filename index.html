CiscoOctober 26, 2026

Cisco IQ Partner Support Goes Generally Available as a Partner-Branded Managed Service

On October 26, Cisco made Cisco IQ Partner Support – Standard generally available so partners can deliver Cisco IQ under their own brand, with their own experts, as a contracted managed service. Liz Centoni’s Cisco blog says more than 16,000 customers already run Cisco IQ across nearly 20 million devices, and the partner edition adds production APIs, an MCP server, and ITSM integrations so partners can embed telemetry into their own workflows and AI agents. New Resilient Infrastructure Services include a Quantum Safe assessment and a Software End of Vulnerability Support assessment. A related Cisco 360 Partner Program update the same day adds ecosystem deal registration and higher adoption incentives, including on Cisco IQ.

DellOctober 27, 2026

Dell Extends Cyber Resilience to AI Environments, Identity Platforms, and Quantum-Safe Storage

On October 27, Dell announced services and product updates aimed at attack surfaces most recovery plans still treat as secondary. Security Hardening for Dell AI Factory and Resilience Operations for AI assess configuration risk and define recovery for critical AI capabilities; PowerProtect AI Assistant adds daily risk briefings with human approval before action. Identity resilience restores clean copies of Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, and Okta, with 24x7 Managed Detection and Response for Identity. On storage, PowerScale gains retention-locked snapshots with SmartSync replication to ObjectScale, and PowerStore adds quantum-safe encryption for data in flight over Fibre Channel with supported PowerEdge servers, plus a Post-Quantum Readiness Assessment.

F5 Relevance

Dell is now treating identity platforms and AI factories as recovery targets, which lines up with F5’s identity-aware access, API security, and certificate lifecycle work. Quantum-safe Fibre Channel encryption and the readiness assessment are a joint story with F5 SSL/TLS termination and post-quantum certificate management in front of PowerStore and AI Factory inference paths.
