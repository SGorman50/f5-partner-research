Nvidia August 8, 2026

Firebird Launches CIS Region's Largest AI Factory in Armenia on NVIDIA DSX with Dell Infrastructure

On August 8, 2026, Firebird opened the CIS region's largest AI factory in Hrazdan, Armenia, powered by NVIDIA accelerated computing (Blackwell and Rubin GPUs) and Dell Technologies high-performance AI infrastructure on the NVIDIA DSX platform with Spectrum-X networking. The site targets more than 70,000 GPUs and 300 MW of AI capacity in Armenia by end-2027, with a broader multi-country roadmap toward ~2 GW. NVIDIA is investing in Firebird to scale AI factories in frontier markets; the facility is positioned for local developers, enterprises, universities, and public institutions.

Dell PowerEdge Underpins Firebird's NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Launch in Armenia

Dell Technologies is the high-performance infrastructure partner behind Firebird's August 8, 2026 launch of the CIS region's largest AI factory in Armenia. The deployment runs on Dell PowerEdge servers within an NVIDIA DSX reference design intended to improve GPU density and energy efficiency (cited up to ~40% more GPUs per footprint). Firebird plans 70,000+ NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs and 300 MW of capacity in Armenia by end-2027, extending Dell's role in rack-scale AI factory rollouts beyond traditional enterprise private AI.
