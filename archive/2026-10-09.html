Accenture and Dell Form a Business Group to Scale Private and Sovereign AI

On October 8, 2026, Accenture and Dell Technologies formed the Accenture Dell Business Group, expanding a 20-year collaboration to help enterprises run agentic AI in private, hybrid, and sovereign environments. The group will offer private AI and neocloud solutions, an integrated private AI stack, and support for open-source and open-weight models, with Accenture training more than 3,000 practitioners on the Dell portfolio. Michael Dell and Accenture CEO Julie Sweet framed the move as taking customers from AI interest to production with more predictable cost and data control. No M&A was reported.

Scality Launches AI Inference Factory for On-Prem Open-Weight Models

On October 8, 2026, Scality announced AI Inference Factory, an open-code stack for running validated open-weight models on enterprise-owned infrastructure. It packages a disaggregated serving layer, a control plane for authentication, metering, routing, and scheduling, and Scality AI Data Infrastructure as a shared KV cache reached over RDMA. Scality says a lab warm time-to-first-token was 166 ms on a 14K-token context, about 14x faster than recompute, and the stack runs on Dell, HPE, Lenovo, and Supermicro servers as a license or managed service. Demonstrated at Scality Day in Paris the same day.

Source

F5 Relevance

A shared, storage-backed KV cache and an inference control plane (auth, metering, routing) is a direct fit for F5 AI traffic management and NGINX/API gateway: token-aware routing, TLS termination, and policy in front of on-prem inference endpoints that must stay sovereign.
