Epic 2026-07-31

Wolters Kluwer Health Partners with Epic to Deliver Integrated Clinical Content Solutions

Wolters Kluwer Health partnered with Epic to embed UpToDate Lexidrug (drug referential content), Medi-Span (medication safety data), and UpToDate Patient Education as a pre-validated clinical content bundle natively inside Epic EHR workflows. The offering targets safety-net and resource-constrained providers—rural hospitals, FQHCs, tribal health systems, state hospitals, and small ambulatory practices—with a single cost center for procurement and reduced IT validation burden. Coverage dated July 31–August 3, 2026 confirms the partnership is live in the current news cycle.

F5 Relevance

Healthcare EHR ecosystems generate high volumes of API, FHIR, and identity traffic between clinical apps, decision-support services, and patient-facing portals. F5 can position BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud for secure API gateway, WAF, and zero-trust access in front of Epic-integrated third-party clinical content services—especially for multi-site safety-net deployments that need consistent app delivery and API security without heavy local IT overhead.
