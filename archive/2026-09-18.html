CrowdStrikeSeptember 17, 2026

CrowdStrike Details SafeMind Adversarial Co-Evolution with NVIDIA Nemotron

On September 17, 2026, CrowdStrike published a technical deep-dive on SafeMind, a closed-loop agentic cybersecurity system from its Cyber Superintelligence Lab. Offense (Red Tempest, a 27-billion-parameter multi-agent model covering 1,000-plus attack scenarios and 155 MITRE ATT&CK techniques) and defense (Blue Solano, using NVIDIA Nemotron Ultra as the reasoning orchestrator and a post-trained Nemotron Super 120B MoE detection model) continuously co-evolve. CrowdStrike claims 70% better accuracy, about 99% lower detection-generation cost, and 6x faster autonomous detection creation on new attacks. The initial offering is FAIRR, powered by Red Tempest, with Blue Solano positioned as a foundation for future products.

F5 Relevance

SafeMind's agentic offense/defense loop and NVIDIA Nemotron runtime sit on the same AI traffic, API, and identity surfaces F5 already secures. Partnership angle: BIG-IP, NGINX, and F5 Distributed Cloud as the inspection and policy layer in front of Falcon/SafeMind agent traffic, plus joint Secure AI stories around MCP and agent gateways.

DellSeptember 16, 2026

Dell and JFrog Validate AI Software Supply-Chain Governance on Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA

On September 16, 2026, Dell published a validated Automation Platform blueprint that deploys the JFrog Platform on Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA. The stack centralizes models, containers, packages (60-plus formats), and binaries behind a governed gateway that proxies public registries, including Hugging Face, with RBAC, SSO, provenance, and signed evidence. The blueprint automates infrastructure configuration, storage integration, secrets management, and lifecycle operations so enterprises can scale AI artifact delivery without letting ungoverned model pulls become a bottleneck.

Source

F5 Relevance

Governed AI software supply chains still need a secure data path for model pulls, registry proxies, and agent artifact delivery. F5 can sit in front of JFrog/Artifactory and Dell AI Factory north-south traffic with WAF, API security, mTLS, and bot/agent identity controls as customers move from AI pilots to production.

EpicSeptember 15, 2026

Epic Hosts Second Agent Factory Build-A-Thon as Health Systems Build In-EHR AI Agents

On September 15, 2026, Epic said 25 health systems are in Verona for the second Agent Factory Build-a-Thon, building end-to-end AI agents inside Epic's Agent Factory platform. Teams are working on agents that flag early signs of necrotizing enterocolitis from notes, reduce day-of-surgery cancellations, protocol-check medication refill requests, and draft missing critical-care documentation. Additional agents work with Art, Epic's clinician AI, including medication-conflict checks and root-cause timelines after safety events. Training for the first early-adopter group starts in October 2026, with broader availability planned for 2027.

Source

F5 Relevance

In-EHR AI agents create new FHIR/API and identity-aware access paths across hospital networks and payer integrations. F5 API gateway, WAF, and zero-trust access are a natural fit for securing Agent Factory traffic between Epic, clinical apps, and external systems without exposing PHI on the public internet.
