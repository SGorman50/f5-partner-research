Red Hat Launches Ansible Development Workspaces on OpenShift Dev Spaces

Developers get a governed workspace in minutes with ansible-lint, the VS Code extension, and team lint profiles preloaded, so source stays on the cluster instead of laptops. Arista Says Etherlink AI Demand Is Broad While Supply Caps 40% Growth Guide

Speaking August 21, 2026 at Rosenblatt’s Age of AI conference, Arista CFO Chantelle Breithaupt said Etherlink AI networking grew from 4–5 customers in 2024 to more than 100, spanning hyperscalers, neoclouds, AI labs, enterprises, and campus. She reiterated a 40% revenue-growth outlook limited by supply, not demand pull-forward. Product VP Brendan Gibbs said EOS software is increasingly important for scale-across (about one-third of guided business), 1.6T trials are underway, and Ethernet scale-up (ESAN via OCP) is a 2028-plus opportunity.

F5 Relevance

Cisco Patches Five Secure Workload Flaws, Including Two CVSS 10.0 Auth Bugs

On August 21, 2026, The Register reported Cisco disclosed five Secure Workload (formerly Tetration) vulnerabilities found in an internal review that used existing tests and frontier AI models. CVE-2026-20315 and CVE-2026-20317 are CVSS 10.0 access-control/authentication issues; others cover command injection (9.9), input validation (9.6), and memory-buffer flaws (7.5). SaaS is patched but customers must still upgrade Agent and Connector; on-prem 3.10 needs 3.10.9.1 and 4.0 needs 4.0.4.16. Cisco said it has seen no exploitation.

Read source

F5 Relevance

Secure Workload is a micro-segmentation control plane adjacent to F5 BIG-IP and XC. Auth-bypass and agent/connector update requirements are a reminder that segmentation APIs need their own WAF, mTLS, and identity controls — and that AI-assisted vuln discovery is now part of how Cisco (and peers) harden products.

Equinix Named a Singapore DC-CFA2 Operator for New AI-Ready Capacity

On August 21–22, 2026, Equinix was named one of four operators selected in Singapore’s second Data Centre Call for Application (DC-CFA2), which will add at least 200 MW of new capacity with green-energy conditions. Intel Outlines Diamond Rapids, Crescent Island, and Wildcat Lake for Agentic AI

On August 24, 2026, Intel used Hot Chips to preview three agentic-AI architectures: Diamond Rapids (next-gen Xeon, up to 256 cores, 128 PCIe Gen6/CXL 3.0 lanes, HBM, enhanced AMX/APX), Crescent Island (air-cooled inference GPU, 32 Xe cores, up to 480GB LPDDR5X, 350W), and Wildcat Lake / Core Series 3 for client and edge Hybrid AI (NPU up to 17 TOPS, first UCIe in an Intel processor). CTO Pushkar Ranade framed the stack as heterogeneous compute plus open chiplets under real-world power and cost constraints.

Read source

F5 Relevance

Crescent Island and Diamond Rapids change rack-level inference density and east-west I/O (PCIe Gen6/CXL). F5 should map BIG-IP, NGINX, and AI traffic management to these air-cooled inference nodes and edge Hybrid AI clients as Intel customers stand up agent runtimes.

NVIDIA Takes Minority Stake in Cloverleaf to Accelerate AI Data-Center Buildout

On August 21, 2026, NVIDIA announced a strategic partnership with Cloverleaf Infrastructure, which secures power and site infrastructure between utilities and data-center operators. Terms were undisclosed; WSJ and Reuters reported a multi-hundred-million-dollar minority stake. Cloverleaf will adopt NVIDIA’s DSX specification so sites can host DSX-compliant AI factories. The deal follows NVIDIA’s $1.5B SB Energy / OpenAI Ohio campus investment earlier the same week.

Read source

F5 Relevance

NVIDIA is financing the power and site layer of AI factories, not just GPUs. F5 should treat DSX-compliant Cloverleaf sites as future Secure AI Factory / AI Guardrails design-win targets — the same traffic, identity, and S3/inference paths F5 already maps to NVIDIA-centric racks.
