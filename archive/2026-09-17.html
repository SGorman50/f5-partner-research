CiscoSeptember 16, 2026

Cisco Patches Actively Exploited ISE Authentication-Bypass Zero-Day (CVE-2026-76460)

On September 16, 2026 Cisco released emergency patches for CVE-2026-76460, a CVSS 10.0 authentication-bypass flaw in Cisco Identity Services Engine and ISE-PIC. An unauthenticated attacker can send crafted requests to an API endpoint that lacks sufficient authentication and bypass the web management interface. Cisco PSIRT confirmed active exploitation; successful attacks can yield root command execution and allow attackers to hide or delete indicators of compromise. There are no workarounds other than restricting management access with infrastructure ACLs. Fixed releases are ISE/ISE-PIC 3.5 Patch 4, 3.4 Patch 7, 3.3 Patch 12, 3.2 Patch 11, and 3.1 Patch 12. CISA added the bug to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and directed U.S. civilian agencies to patch within three days.

SecurityWeek — Cisco ISE zero-day CVE-2026-76460

F5 Relevance

ISE sits in the NAC and identity-enforcement path that F5 Access Policy Manager, BIG-IP, and Distributed Cloud often front or complement. Joint customers will need ADC/WAF policy updates, management-plane isolation, and identity-aware access controls while they patch, and F5 can help segment ISE admin interfaces and inspect east-west API traffic to the vulnerable endpoint.

CiscoSeptember 15, 2026

Cisco Integrates KETS Chip-Based QKD into IOS XR for Quantum-Safe MACsec

Computer Weekly reported on September 15, 2026 that Cisco has integrated chip-based Quantum Key Distribution from UK firm KETS Quantum Security into its IOS XR routing infrastructure. The demonstration uses KETS QKD v2.0 to automatically exchange quantum-safe keys with Cisco routers and feed them into MACsec for end-to-end encryption on existing Ethernet networks, without a forklift upgrade. Cisco has supported hardware QKD in IOS XR since 7.9.1 (2023); this step moves chip-scale photonic QKD toward practical metro and WAN deployment for enterprise, finance, telecom, and government networks. Both companies plan broader use cases and deeper hardware integration.

Computer Weekly — Cisco / KETS quantum-safe networks

F5 Relevance

Quantum-safe keying on the WAN and MACsec edge maps directly to F5 TLS, certificate lifecycle, and post-quantum cryptography work. F5 can terminate or pass through PQC and QKD-backed sessions on BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud in front of IOS XR fabrics, and jointly position a quantum-ready application delivery layer for customers preparing crypto-agile WAN designs.

CrowdStrikeSeptember 16, 2026

CrowdStrike Adds On-Device AI Data Classification to Falcon with Intel NPUs

On September 16, 2026 CrowdStrike said Falcon Data Security now classifies unstructured sensitive data using language models that run on-device on dedicated AI hardware, starting with Intel Core Ultra AI PCs. Joint engineering with Intel produced OpenVINO-supported, NPU-optimized models so classification of documents, chat, tickets, and AI prompts happens locally without sending content to the cloud. CrowdStrike uses ONNX Runtime with the OpenVINO Execution Provider so the same model can target NPU, GPU, or CPU as endpoint silicon evolves. The NPU path is preferred for always-on protection because it is lower power and does not contend with graphics workloads.

CrowdStrike — On-device AI data classification

F5 Relevance

On-device classification of credentials and AI prompts is an endpoint counterpart to F5 inspection of data in flight at the ADC, WAF, and API gateway. F5 can pair Falcon Data Security signals with SSL/TLS orchestration and API security so sensitive content identified on the device is also controlled as it traverses BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud, especially in AI-PC and hybrid-work architectures.

Nutanix Named a Leader in Gartner 2026 Magic Quadrant for Server Virtualization

On September 16, 2026 Nutanix announced it was named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Server Virtualization Platforms, positioned highest for Ability to Execute. Nutanix Cloud Infrastructure with the built-in AHV hypervisor (KVM-based) is the foundation of Nutanix Cloud Platform, unifying compute, storage, networking, and virtualization for VMs, containers, and AI workloads across hybrid and sovereign environments. Product VP Ketan Shah framed the recognition around running agentic AI at scale without rigid all-or-nothing virtualization bundles, citing modular licensing and broader hardware and storage choice including Dell and NetApp options.

Nutanix — Gartner MQ Leader for server virtualization

Emerald AI, Google, and NVIDIA Launch AI Energy Management Alliance

On September 16, 2026 Emerald AI, Google, and NVIDIA launched the AI Energy Management Alliance (AEMA) to make AI data centers a flexible, grid-responsive load rather than a static interconnect. The coalition will define performance-based flexibility metrics (response speed, duration, ride-through, curtailment) and work with utilities and grid operators on faster, risk-adjusted interconnection for AI factories. NVIDIA said it is already working with Emerald AI on factories that can shift compute, discharge storage, or use paired generation when the grid is constrained, and AEMA will broaden that model across the U.S. power and AI value chain.

NVIDIA Blog — AI Energy Management Alliance

F5 Relevance

Grid-flexible AI factories will still need deterministic, secure application and inference traffic as workloads shift in real time. F5 AI traffic management, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud can sit in front of those elastic GPU clusters to keep APIs, KV-cache paths, and north-south inference flows available and protected when power-driven load shedding or workload migration occurs.

Red HatSeptember 16, 2026

Red Hat and Duality Detail Sovereign AI on OpenShift Confidential Containers

On September 16, 2026 Red Hat and Duality Technologies published a joint architecture for sovereign AI that combines Duality privacy-enhancing technologies with Red Hat OpenShift confidential containers. Workloads run in hardware TEEs (Intel TDX or AMD SEV) so data stays encrypted even if the host OS or hypervisor is compromised, with hardware attestation before code executes. Duality federated learning keeps raw datasets local while models train across contributors. OpenShift sandboxed containers (kata-cc) and the Red Hat build of Trustee act as the verifier and key broker, releasing keys only after quotes check out. The pattern targets regulated and national-sovereignty AI that cannot land on a public cloud.

Red Hat — Sovereign AI with Duality

F5 Relevance

Confidential-container AI still needs a trusted ingress, API, and TLS edge. F5 can terminate or pass through attested TLS in front of OpenShift TEEs, enforce identity-aware access to Duality aggregators, and jointly position BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud as the sovereign front door for privacy-preserving inference and federated-learning control planes.
