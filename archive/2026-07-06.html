Arista Networks Reports 35% Revenue Growth, Raises AI Networking Guidance to $3.5B

Arista Networks posted 35% YoY revenue growth and raised its AI networking revenue target to $3.5B, with deferred revenue up $826M and a customer NPS of 89, signaling strong enterprise commitment to its AI fabric portfolio. HPE Advances GreenLake Virtualization Strategy to Meet Rising AI Infrastructure Demands

HPE is pushing a phased, hybrid virtualization modernization strategy through GreenLake, aimed at lowering TCO, shifting CAPEX to OPEX, and preparing infrastructure for AI workloads. Intel July 4, 2026

Intel Doubles Down on Efficient AI Inference Strategy as Alternative to GPU Training Arms Race

Intel is positioning Xeon CPUs paired with partner accelerators (e.g., SambaNova SN-50 RDUs) as an energy-efficient inference alternative to Nvidia's GPU-dominated training market, shifting its target CPU-to-GPU ratio from 1:8 toward 1:4. The strategy aims to stabilize data-center revenue as AMD, Google TPU, and AWS Trainium intensify custom-silicon competition.

F5 Relevance
A more CPU-centric, distributed inference architecture increases east-west and inference-endpoint traffic that needs intelligent load balancing -- directly aligned with F5's AI inference optimization and traffic management roadmap.
Nutanix July 2-3, 2026

Nutanix Launches Agent Gateway for Centralized Governance of Enterprise AI Agents

Nutanix announced general availability of Agent Gateway within Nutanix Enterprise AI 2.7, a control plane governing interactions between AI agents, LLMs, and enterprise tools across public, private, and self-hosted environments. Features include access policies, tool-level filtering over Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, unified observability/audit logs, and token-based rate limiting across a Kubernetes-based inference pipeline.

F5 Relevance
Agent Gateway's access-policy enforcement, MCP tool-level filtering, and token-based rate limiting over Kubernetes inference pipelines overlaps directly with F5's API gateway and AI traffic management portfolio -- a strong integration/partnership angle for securing agentic AI traffic at the network layer.
