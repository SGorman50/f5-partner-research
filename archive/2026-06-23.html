NetApp Enhances Support for AI Workloads at Massive Scale — StorageGRID 12.1

NetApp announced StorageGRID 12.1, delivering a globally federated namespace that enables management of multiple distributed StorageGRID systems at up to 10 exabytes in a single namespace. The release delivers up to 12 TB/s throughput to AI factories — up to 400% higher than 12.0 — with batch operations on billions of objects and improved AI data pipeline tracking. Expanded security and governance controls include multi-admin verification for regulated environments. Forrester recognizes NetApp as a leader in object storage.

F5 Relevance
StorageGRID 12.1’s exabyte-scale federated namespace and 12 TB/s throughput for AI factories create a major S3 data-flow surface where F5 can deliver secure, high-performance delivery and load balancing between AI compute clusters and object storage. The multi-admin verification and S3 API governance features align with F5’s API security and zero-trust positioning.

NVIDIA Announces Halos for Robotics — Industry’s First Full-Stack Safety System for Physical AI

NVIDIA announced Halos for Robotics, the first full-stack safety architecture for physical AI spanning AI compute (NVIDIA IGX Thor), sensors (Holoscan Sensor Bridge), safety software (Halos OS), and certification support (Halos AI Systems Inspection Lab). The system extends NVIDIA’s autonomous-vehicle safety framework to factory, warehouse, and logistics robotics. Agility is the first innovator to integrate Halos for Robotics into its safety system.

F5 Relevance
As NVIDIA Halos for Robotics scales physical AI deployments in industrial settings, the real-time data flows between IGX Thor compute, sensor networks, and cloud-based AI orchestration layers represent a new edge-to-cloud traffic pattern. F5’s AI traffic management and application security capabilities are well-positioned to secure and optimize these latency-sensitive inference paths.

Europe Unveils a Record 35 New NVIDIA AI Supercomputers

NVIDIA announced at ISC High Performance 2026 (June 22) that a record 35 new NVIDIA AI supercomputers are being deployed across 23 European countries, reaching more than 3 million researchers. The systems — including the Barcelona Supercomputing Center EuroHPC AI Factory, BavariaAI Blue Swan, and NAISS Mimer — run on NVIDIA Blackwell and Hopper architectures with Quantum InfiniBand networking, NIM microservices, and NVIDIA AI Enterprise. NVIDIA AI infrastructure powers over 90% of Europe’s AI factory buildout, with ~800 AI exaflops deployed.

F5 Relevance
35 new AI supercomputers deploying NVIDIA Quantum InfiniBand and Blackwell/Hopper at European national labs creates a large new addressable market for F5’s AI traffic management, NGINX, and distributed cloud capabilities to secure and optimize the API and inference traffic between AI factories and enterprise consumers. The multi-country sovereign AI deployments highlight demand for F5’s zero-trust and data sovereignty-aware delivery.
