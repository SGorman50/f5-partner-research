CrowdStrike Falcon Next-Gen SIEM Joins CISA SIEMaaS Stack

CrowdStrike said on October 1, 2026 that Falcon Next-Gen SIEM is now in CISA's SIEMaaS technology stack under the CDM DEFEND F shared service. Eligible federal agencies already on CrowdStrike EDR can extend SOC coverage through CISA's Persistent Access Capability program without spending their own program funds, with CGI Federal acquiring the SKUs on their behalf. The move is a funded path to federal SOC modernization rather than a new product launch.

Source

F5 Relevance. Federal SOC modernization on Falcon Next-Gen SIEM creates a shared telemetry and response plane. F5 can pair that with BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud WAF/API security in front of agency apps so identity, application, and SIEM signals line up in the same operating model.

Dell Technologies Capital Leads Ascerta's $18M Series A for AI ROI Tracking

Dell Technologies Capital led an $18 million Series A for Ascerta (formerly Pay-i), reported September 30, 2026, with Hitachi Ventures, BGV, and Wipro Ventures also participating. Ascerta's Enterprise AI Management platform gives CIOs and CFOs one view of AI cost, adoption, and business value so they can see which deployments pay off. Total funding is now about $22.9 million. This is a venture investment by Dell's capital arm, not an acquisition of Dell.

Source

F5 Relevance. Enterprises adding an AI cost-and-value system of record still need a delivery and security layer under the workloads being measured. F5 AI traffic management, NGINX, and API security can sit in front of those inference endpoints so token cost, TLS, and policy are visible alongside Ascerta-style ROI tracking.
