Arista 7060XE7 Series: 1.6 Tbps AI Fabric Portfolio Goes Into Production

Arista announced its 7060XE7 Series, a next-generation 1.6 Tbps Ethernet switch portfolio based on Broadcom Tomahawk 6 silicon, designed for rack-scale AI infrastructure. The portfolio supports both scale-out and scale-up AI workloads with 224G SerDes and 100 Tbps switching capacity. A liquid-cooled, ORv3 open-rack design is included, and Arista's EtherLink capabilities are expanding with open-standard compute fabrics targeting GPU networking.
