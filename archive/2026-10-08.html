AristaOctober 7, 2026

Arista Announces Open Ethernet Rack-Scale Architectures for Scale-Up and Scale-Out AI Fabrics

On October 7, Arista announced an open Ethernet rack-scale portfolio for high-density scale-up and scale-out AI fabrics, engineered with AMD, Arm, Broadcom, d-Matrix, Meta, Microsoft, and Qualcomm under the Ethernet for Scale-up Networks (ESUN) initiative. Liquid-cooled racks support up to 144 accelerators per rack and larger domains via cross-rack architecture, positioning Ethernet as a rack-scale backplane rather than a pipe between servers. Arista is showing the designs at OCP Global Summit (October 12–15).

Scale-up Ethernet fabrics between accelerators are a new delivery and telemetry surface next to F5's AI traffic management and NGINX ingress. A joint story with Arista on securing and observing east-west AI fabric traffic, including multi-plane load balancing into inference clusters, is a concrete partnership angle.

Cisco's October 7 NX-OS Cycle Includes Unauthenticated Remote Code Execution on Nexus Switches

Cisco published its October 7, 2026 NX-OS security advisories, including critical unauthenticated remote code execution in Next Generation OAM on Nexus 3000 and 9000 switches (CVE-2026-76485, CVE-2026-76486, and CVE-2026-76501) when NGOAM is enabled, plus a separate NX-API RCE and an MPLS OAM RCE. A companion hardening release covers additional high-severity flaws, some scored 9.8, across MDS, Nexus, and UCS Fabric Interconnects. Cisco says there are no workarounds other than disabling unused features and upgrading; Live Protect shields are offered as interim mitigation.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Nexus fabrics often sit under the same AI and application clusters F5 fronts. A joint advisory narrative — F5 WAF and BIG-IP policy in front of management and data-plane exposure while customers patch NX-OS — is a practical alliance motion for shared Cisco-F5 accounts.

DellOctober 5, 2026

Dell Patches 18 Critical Flaws in Container Storage Modules and System Update

CSO Online reported October 5 that Dell disclosed 18 CVEs in Dell Container Storage Modules and Dell System Update, including two CVSS 10 authentication bypasses. Unauthenticated attackers could bypass auth, gain root, steal storage-admin credentials, forge tokens, or run code. CSM versions before 1.17.0 and DSU before 2.3.0.0 are affected; Dell says there are no workarounds and no evidence of active exploitation. This is distinct from the earlier Health-ISAC note on the same CSM family.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

CSM is the link between Dell storage and Kubernetes, the same path F5 NGINX Ingress and BIG-IP often front. A joint patch-and-protect note for shared storage-plus-ingress customers is a timely security motion while arrays are upgraded.

HPE Launches ProLiant Gen13 Servers on AMD EPYC 9006 for AI Inference

Network World reported October 7 that HPE is rolling out four ProLiant Gen13 models anchored on AMD's sixth-generation EPYC 9006 (Venice) processors. The 10U DL585a supports up to eight double-wide GPUs and two EPYC CPUs over PCIe Gen6 for inference, RAG fine-tuning, and agentic AI; a 1U DL525 targets high-throughput inference and analytics; liquid-cooled XD245 and air-cooled XD285 are rack-scale designs. All include iLO 8 with expanded automated security. Shipping is staggered from later 2026 into 2027.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Gen13 is the CPU head-node and GPU host layer under GreenLake private cloud AI. F5 can position BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud as the secure ingress and API path into these inference nodes, especially where iLO and PCIe Gen6 clusters sit behind customer WAFs.
