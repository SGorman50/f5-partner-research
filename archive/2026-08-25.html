Cisco Expands Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA for the Rack-Scale Era

On August 25, 2026, Cisco expanded Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA through a partnership with Supermicro, adding liquid- and air-cooled rack-scale GPU systems to its full-stack secure AI architecture. The offering is NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP) compliant, pairing Cisco Silicon One front-end and Spectrum-X back-end switches under Nexus One, and targets NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 and HGX Rubin NVL8 for trillion-parameter training through edge inference. Cisco Validated Infrastructure Services (CVIS), aligned with NVIDIA Infrastructure Services, is intended to certify deployments against those reference architectures.

https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m08/cisco-secure-ai-factory-nvidia-rack-scale.html

F5 Relevance

Cisco is productizing Secure AI Factory as a rack-to-fabric NVIDIA stack for enterprises, neoclouds, and sovereign clouds. F5 should map AI Guardrails, BIG-IP, and NGINX to the same NCP/sovereign-cloud buying motion — front-end API and inference traffic, identity-aware access, and S3/KV-cache data paths that sit adjacent to Nexus One fabrics.

Lancium Partners with NVIDIA to Deploy Gigawatt-Scale AI Factories Across 15+ GW Portfolio

On August 24, 2026, Lancium announced a strategic collaboration and NVIDIA investment to place NVIDIA’s full-stack AI factory platform — accelerated computing, networking, and software — across 4 GW of leased capacity and a 15+ GW powered-land pipeline. Campuses will use NVIDIA DSX reference designs, including DSX MaxLPS (up to 40% more GPUs in the same power budget) and DSX Flex for grid-responsive power. Blackstone-backed Lancium positions the sites as power-ready capacity for cloud partners, AI-native firms, and other NVIDIA ecosystem customers.

https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lancium-announces-partnership-with-nvidia-to-advance-gigawatt-scale-ai-factory-development-across-its-15-gw-portfolio-302858393.html

F5 Relevance

NVIDIA is again locking the power-and-site layer of AI factories, not just GPUs. F5 should treat Lancium DSX campuses as Secure AI Factory / AI Guardrails design-win targets for east-west GPU fabric traffic, north-south inference APIs, and S3/object data paths into those racks.

August 24, 2026

SpaceXAI Adopts NVIDIA Vera CPU to Accelerate Agentic AI at Massive Scale

On August 24, 2026, NVIDIA announced that SpaceXAI will deploy NVIDIA Vera CPUs — the first CPU built for AI agents — to orchestrate tools, execute code, and keep GPUs utilized between model calls. SpaceXAI is expanding Grok infrastructure on the Vera Rubin platform toward gigawatts of capacity and plans a first-generation Starmind AI satellite based on an optimized Vera Rubin NVL72 system.

https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spacexai-adopts-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-accelerate-agentic-ai-at-massive-scale

F5 Relevance

Agentic orchestration on Vera CPUs plus Rubin GPUs increases API, tool-call, and inference traffic that needs identity, rate control, and inspection. F5 can position AI traffic management and AI Guardrails in front of agent runtimes and ground/satellite inference paths in NVIDIA-centric agentic factories.
