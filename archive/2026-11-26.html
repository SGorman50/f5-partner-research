AMD, Cisco, and Humain Sign a Middle East AI Data Center Joint Venture

AK&M reported on November 25, 2026, citing an AMD press release, that AMD, Cisco, and Saudi AI company Humain signed a joint venture to build data centers in the Middle East. The venture starts with a 100 MW project in Saudi Arabia, with Cisco supplying network equipment and AMD supplying MI450 AI chips, and targets up to 1 GW by 2030. AMD and Cisco hold minority stakes and share income and expenses. Humain has also agreed to supply compute to Luma AI.

Equinix Opens Fabric One Preview with Encrypted AWS Interconnect

Equinix published on November 25, 2026 that Fabric One is in public preview with AWS Interconnect endpoints in Silicon Valley and Ashburn. The service uses the OpenAPI 3.0 Interconnect specification so private links can be requested in code, including later by an AI agent, without Equinix taking AWS credentials. Endpoints default to four connections, stay on Equinix's private network, and include MACsec. The same preview can also attach Direct Connect, ExpressRoute, Google Partner Interconnect, and Oracle FastConnect.

Source

F5 Relevance

API-driven private multicloud links are the path AI traffic will take between Equinix and AWS. F5 Distributed Cloud and BIG-IP can sit on those Fabric One edges to load-balance, inspect, and apply WAF and DDoS controls without breaking the private, encrypted path.

Ensign InfoSecurity Partners with HPE on Sovereign Private Cloud AI Security

Ensign InfoSecurity announced on November 26, 2026 a non-binding memorandum with HPE to run AI-powered security operations in customer-controlled environments. NvidiaNovember 25, 2026

Kioxia and NVIDIA Extend GPU Memory Toward Petabyte Scale with SSDs

Kioxia announced on November 25, 2026 a collaboration with NVIDIA that uses NVIDIA SCADA software and Kioxia NVMe SSDs to extend effective GPU memory toward petabyte scale, in support of NVIDIA's Storage-Next initiative. A proof of concept on a GB200 NVL72 rack with 144 Kioxia XD8 7.68 TB SSDs showed about 1.1 PB of raw SSD capacity and the ability to process a 2-trillion-vector database. The design aims to cut repeated movement of AI datasets between high-bandwidth memory and slower storage.

Source

F5 Relevance

Stretching GPU memory into petabyte-scale NVMe increases east-west data movement into AI racks. F5 can secure and accelerate those object and storage flows, including S3-style data paths, so inference clusters stay fed without opening a new exposure on the storage network.
