Cisco Launches Sovereign Critical Infrastructure in Canada

On August 25, 2026, Cisco launched its Sovereign Critical Infrastructure portfolio in Canada, the first market outside EMEA to receive the offering. The configurable stack covers routing, switching, wireless, compute, collaboration, and Splunk security and observability, and can run fully on-premises or air-gapped with customer-controlled licensing so Cisco cannot remotely access or disable systems. It is IPv6-ready, FIPS 140-2/3 and Common Criteria certified, and aligned with Canada's ITSG-33 framework to support Authority to Operate for government, financial, healthcare, and other critical-infrastructure customers adopting AI on their own terms. Bell is a lead partner.

https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m08/cisco-launches-sovereign-critical-infrastructure-in-canada-government-and-organizations-gain-more-choice-and-control.html

F5 Relevance

Air-gapped and sovereign deployments still need application delivery, API security, TLS/PKI, and identity-aware access that can operate without cloud reach-back. F5 can position BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud as complementary on-prem and air-gapped controls for Canadian SCI customers who need to inspect and load-balance AI and mission-critical traffic inside the same sovereign boundary.

3verest Builds Sovereign AI for Healthcare with Equinix Fabric Geo Zones

On August 25, 2026, Equinix announced that sovereign healthcare cloud provider 3verest is using Equinix Fabric Geo Zones to keep medical imaging data, AI inference, prompts, and network traffic inside national borders across Australia, the UK, Germany, the US, Canada, and the EU. Unlike storage-only residency, Fabric Geo Zones enforce sovereignty at the network layer even during failover or rerouting, privately interconnecting hospitals, OEMs, and clouds through Equinix IBX facilities. 3verest already supports more than 200 Equinix customers across 21 markets.

https://newsroom.equinix.com/2026-08-25-3verest-Builds-Sovereign-AI-for-Healthcare-with-Equinix-Fabric-Geo-Zones

F5 Relevance

Sovereign AI traffic still needs in-country inspection, API gateways, and WAF/DDoS in front of imaging and inference endpoints. F5 can pair with Equinix Fabric and 3verest to deliver identity-aware, jurisdiction-bound application and API security for healthcare AI without sending traffic out of the Geo Zone.

NVIDIA Announces Jetson Orin Nano 2 Robotics Computer for Entry-Level Edge AI

On August 25, 2026, NVIDIA announced Jetson Orin Nano 2, an entry-level robotics computer with 78 TOPS, 8GB of memory, and an 8-core Arm CPU that delivers 2x the inference performance of Orin Nano Super in the same form factor and 40% less power at equivalent performance. It is aimed at robots, delivery and inspection drones, and vision AI, running memory-efficient LLMs and VLMs including NVIDIA Cosmos, Nemotron, Gemma 4, and Qwen 3. Early explorers include Cognex, Doosan Bobcat, and Matic, with ecosystem partners building carrier boards and reference systems; more than 3 million developers already use the Jetson stack.

https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-jetson-orin-nano-2-robotics-computer-to-redefine-entry-level-edge-ai

F5 Relevance

Edge robotics and vision agents generate API, telemetry, and model-update traffic that still needs identity, rate control, and inspection between devices, factories, and cloud. F5 can extend AI traffic management and API security to Jetson-class fleets as they connect back to enterprise inference and data planes.
