Equinix June 19, 2026

Equinix Trials Landmark Hydrogen Power Solution at Dublin DB3 Data Centre

Equinix announced a 12-week hydrogen power trial at its Dublin DB3 data center (June 19, 2026) in collaboration with ESB and GeoPura. Two hydrogen-powered generators provide up to 0.5 MW continuous power for cooling with zero direct onsite emissions, achieving a PUE below 1.3. This is Equinix's first hydrogen deployment at scale across its 280+ global data centers.

🔍 F5 Relevance

F5 Distributed Cloud Services leverage Equinix infrastructure for global points of presence and benefit directly from Equinix's power resilience investments. This hydrogen power trial signals Equinix's commitment to sustainable, grid-resilient colocation that underpins F5's edge delivery and interconnection ecosystem.
