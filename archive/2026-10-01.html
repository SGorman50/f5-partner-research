CiscoSeptember 30, 2026

Cisco Patches Actively Exploited SD-WAN Manager Auth Bypass (CVE-2026-76504)

On September 30, 2026, Cisco warned that Catalyst SD-WAN Manager (formerly vManage) is under active attack via CVE-2026-76504, a critical API authentication bypass. Improper URI encoding lets an unauthenticated remote attacker reach the API as admin. Cisco has fixed releases for on-prem and cloud-managed deployments and says there is no workaround. BleepingComputer reported the advisory the same day.

F5 Relevance. SD-WAN controllers are a control plane for branch and multi-cloud traffic that F5 customers also secure with BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud. A live admin-level bypass is a joint incident-response and zero-trust talking point, and a reason to position F5 access controls in front of management APIs that must not be internet-exposed.
CrowdStrikeSeptember 29, 2026

CrowdStrike Details ClickFix Social Engineering and Browser-Side Stops

On September 29, 2026, CrowdStrike published a threat-intel post on ClickFix, a lure that asks a user to paste a command to fix a fake meeting or CAPTCHA. The company says it has seen STARDUST CHOLLIMA and VOODOO BEAR use the technique, and points to Falcon Seraphic Enterprise Browser as the control that ties browser activity to endpoint, identity, and SOC telemetry.

F5 Relevance. ClickFix executes in the browser before it ever hits an endpoint agent. F5 can pair that story with BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud controls that inspect and block malicious redirect and command-paste pages at the application edge, especially for customers already using CrowdStrike for endpoint.

SiliconANGLE coverage of Dell's AI Leadership Symposium (September 29–30, 2026, San Jose) reports four production themes: rack-scale AI rollouts, voice models moving to air-gapped AI PCs with Deepgram, runtime governance of agents inside the customer's VPC, and token-cost pressure pushing inference back on-prem and onto CPUs.
