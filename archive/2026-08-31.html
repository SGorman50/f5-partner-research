CrowdStrike and Telkom Indonesia Enter MoU to Advance AI-Powered Cybersecurity

On August 28, 2026, CrowdStrike and PT Telkom Indonesia signed a memorandum of understanding to accelerate AI-powered cybersecurity transformation in Indonesia. The collaboration aims to standardize Telkom's defenses on the Falcon platform, co-develop managed security offerings for the Indonesian market, and stand up a joint team focused on securing AI agents, models, and systems — including Falcon AI Detection and Response (AIDR) for runtime visibility and enforcement. The MoU is an early-stage partnership with no disclosed financial terms or deployment scope, combining CrowdStrike's AI-native security stack with Telkom's local reach and infrastructure.
