NutanixSeptember 22, 2026

Nutanix Acquires Ryax Technologies to Accelerate Agentic AI on Kubernetes

On September 22, 2026, Nutanix announced it acquired France-based Ryax Technologies, an AI-driven compute orchestration and management platform. Nutanix plans to fold Ryax GPU utilization and smart scheduling into Nutanix Kubernetes Platform (NKP) and Nutanix Enterprise AI (NAI) so enterprises can place agentic inference across private data centers, hyperscalers, and neoclouds without rewriting workloads. The Ryax team will join Nutanix in France; financial terms were not disclosed and the company said the deal is not material. Coverage from CRN, Blocks & Files, and SDxCentral frames it as a tuck-in to make hybrid GPU capacity economically usable for agentic AI.

Source: Nutanix press release

F5 Relevance

Ryax plus NKP/NAI puts more agentic inference on Kubernetes with GPU-aware scheduling across hybrid clouds. That traffic still needs ingress, API gateway, and east-west controls — F5 BIG-IP, NGINX Ingress, and Distributed Cloud can sit in front of NAI/Agent Gateway and GPU worker pools as customers scale multi-site agent runtimes.

CiscoSeptember 22, 2026

Cisco Talos Details CLOSEDQUORUM, an LLM-as-C2 Credential Stealer, and Opens CAIRN

On September 22, 2026, Cisco Talos published research on CLOSEDQUORUM, a 64-bit Windows Go implant it describes as the first reported autonomous AI command-and-control design. The sample queries up to four commercial LLMs (DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, Google Gemini), tallies JSON verdicts, and executes credential-theft steps against LSASS, browser password stores, and crypto wallets without a live operator. Talos said it has not confirmed in-the-wild deployment. The same day it released CAIRN, an open-source toolkit for hunting AI-integrated malware via API, prompt, framework, and runtime filters. CSO Online and WIRED also covered the work.

Source: Cisco Talos Blog

F5 Relevance

LLM-as-C2 turns outbound model API calls into the control channel. F5 API security, SSL/TLS inspection, and AI traffic management can detect unexpected Windows processes calling DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Mistral, or Gemini, enforce egress policy, and inspect structured prompts — a concrete joint story with Cisco Secure/Talos customers running F5 at the edge.

Arista Positions 7800 AI Spine for Scale-Across Fabrics Between Distant GPU Clusters

On September 22, 2026, Arista CEO Jayshree Ullal and Brendan Gibbs published a strategy blog arguing that power and space limits force AI training off a single campus into geographic scale-across fabrics. The piece casts the Arista 7800 as the spine for traffic isolation, SRv6 multiplane forwarding, multi-tenant traffic engineering, and tens-of-microseconds recovery across regions, with unified EOS from scale-up through long-haul coherent optics. Source: Arista blog

F5 can complement Arista spines with BIG-IP/SSL offload, API gateway, and Distributed Cloud security where agent and inference flows leave the Ethernet fabric.

NVIDIA Forms Applied AI Architecture Group in Israel for Genomics and Materials

On September 23, 2026, bne IntelliNews, citing Calcalist, reported that NVIDIA has stood up an Applied AI Architecture group at its Israel development center under CTO Michael Kagan, led by former Intel and Huawei executive Nati Daniel. Red HatSeptember 21, 2026

Red Hat Discloses Important oc-mirror PGP Bypass in Disconnected OpenShift (CVE-2026-75939)

On September 21, 2026, Red Hat published CVE-2026-75939 (Important, CVSS 7.4) in OpenShift’s oc-mirror tool. The utility checks OpenPGP signature errors before the signed body is fully consumed, so a network attacker who can intercept the signature endpoint can present a message with a valid Red Hat release key ID and a forged signature. oc-mirror may then accept and mirror a malicious release payload into a disconnected registry. Red Hat said no practical mitigation currently meets its product-security bar. Rapid7, Tenable, and independent write-ups mirrored the advisory the same day.

Source: Red Hat Customer Portal

F5 Relevance

Disconnected OpenShift customers pull release images through mirrors that sit behind load balancers and TLS intercept. F5 can add signed-image path controls, restrict signature-endpoint exposure, and WAF/API-protect registry and oc-mirror traffic — a timely OpenShift supply-chain story for joint Red Hat accounts.
