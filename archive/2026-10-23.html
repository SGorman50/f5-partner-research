NVIDIA Is Reportedly in Talks to Invest Hundreds of Millions in Firmus Grid

The Information reported that NVIDIA is in talks to invest hundreds of millions of dollars in Firmus Grid, an Australian AI cloud (neocloud) provider, after Firmus scrapped an IPO that had targeted a valuation above $30 billion. NVIDIA already holds a stake in Firmus and in similar AI cloud companies. The talks are reported, not confirmed by NVIDIA.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

A larger NVIDIA stake in a neocloud means more GPU-as-a-service traffic that still needs secure ingress, API gateways, and tenant isolation. F5 Distributed Cloud and NGINX are a fit for the north-south path into Firmus-style GPU clouds, alongside the OpenShell runtime NVIDIA is already pushing.

Cisco
October 21, 2026

Cisco and NVIDIA Publish an AI Inference Routing Blueprint for Service Providers

Cisco published an AI Inference Routing blueprint that treats distributed edge and regional capacity as one system. Using SRv6, Flex-Algo, and BGP, providers can place, route, secure, and assure inference based on latency, cost, and jurisdiction, with network slicing so inference traffic stays isolated. Cisco also noted Fortanix Confidential AI now supports CNCF Confidential Containers the same day.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Inference routing is an application-delivery problem. F5 BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud can sit in front of these distributed inference sites as the policy, TLS, and API-security layer, while NGINX can enforce per-tenant routing that the WAN blueprint assumes but does not terminate.

October 21, 2026

Schneider Electric, NVIDIA, and HPE Build Industrial AI Agents at the Edge

Schneider Electric, NVIDIA, and HPE announced Industrial AI Agents at the Edge, co-engineered on HPE Private Cloud AI and NVIDIA technology. The agents run near process and heavy-industry operations for OT monitoring, cybersecurity, and energy optimization, with a demonstration at CMPC in Chile. The same day HPE also expanded its AI Factory with NVIDIA around OpenShell and a Vera CPU rack.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Industrial edge agents need the same API, identity, and OT/IT segmentation controls as data-center inference. F5 can offer BIG-IP and NGINX as the policy enforcement point between plant-floor agents and enterprise systems, including WAF and mTLS for those agent-to-system calls.

Equinix
October 21, 2026

Equinix Opens EPIC Liquid-Cooling Innovation Centers for High-Density AI

Equinix launched Enhanced Performance Innovation Centers (EPIC), production-ready liquid-cooling labs inside live IBX data centers. Sites are live in Frankfurt, London, Osaka, Paris, and Sydney, with more planned for 2027. Customers can test liquid-cooled GPU and CPU platforms, power, networking, and operations before production. Criteo is an early user.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

EPIC is where high-density AI fabrics get validated before production. F5 can position BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud as the secure ingress and multicloud interconnect layer inside those same IBX halls, especially where inference traffic crosses Equinix Fabric.
