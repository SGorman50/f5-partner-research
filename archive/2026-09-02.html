September 1, 2026

CrowdStrike Launches Frontier Models for Cybersecurity, Created with NVIDIA

At Fal.Con 2026 on September 1, CrowdStrike unveiled SafeMind, a family of frontier agentic cybersecurity models built with NVIDIA Nemotron and trained on CrowdStrike telemetry, threat intel, and incident-response data. SafeMind ships natively in the Falcon platform as a closed-loop offensive and defensive agentic stack rather than a copilot, with CrowdStrike citing about 29% higher detection, 6x faster end-to-end remediation, and about 99% cost savings versus leading frontier and open-source baselines. NVIDIA published a companion Fal.Con post describing a digital-twin test environment used to validate the models.

Source

F5 Relevance

SafeMind and Nemotron-based agentic SOC workflows will generate high-volume API, model, and agent-to-agent traffic that needs identity-aware access, WAF/API security, and AI traffic management. F5 can sit in front of Falcon-connected inference and NVIDIA OpenShell runtimes as the delivery and policy layer for agentic security stacks.

Crowdstrike

August 31, 2026

CrowdStrike Brings CLEAR's Verified Human Identity into the Falcon Platform

On August 31, 2026, CrowdStrike and CLEAR announced a partnership that feeds CLEAR1 verified-human identity into the Falcon platform. When Falcon flags risk, CLEAR1 verifies the person in real time and returns the result into Falcon Next-Gen SIEM and Charlotte Agentic SOAR so teams can allow, investigate, or block. The integration is available now to existing CrowdStrike and CLEAR1 customers and is aimed at reducing identity-based attacks by tying high-assurance person verification to Falcon risk signals.

Source

F5 Relevance

Binding high-assurance human identity to session risk is adjacent to F5 identity-aware access, SAML/OAuth/OIDC, and BIG-IP/Distributed Cloud access policies. Joint stories around verified-human step-up at the application edge are a natural alliance conversation.

Dell

September 1, 2026

Dell Technologies Delivers Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results

On September 1, 2026, Dell reported record Q2 FY2027 revenue of $47.0 billion, up 58% year over year, with Infrastructure Solutions Group revenue up 89% to $31.8 billion on AI-optimized servers. Dell raised full-year FY27 revenue guidance to $192 billion and AI-optimized server revenue to $74 billion, citing a $95 billion AI server backlog and $60.9 billion of AI server orders in the quarter. Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $7.04. Management pointed to broad demand across neoclouds, sovereigns, and enterprises, with NVIDIA-powered servers a primary driver.

Source

F5 Relevance

Record NVIDIA-powered AI server shipments expand the front-end and back-end Ethernet, ingress, and API security surface in Dell AI Factory deployments—where F5 BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud already belong as the secure delivery layer.

Epic

September 1, 2026

ChatGPT Health Adds Epic Integration for Clinicians to Import Patient Data

On September 1, 2026, TechCrunch reported that OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Health with Epic EHR integration so clinicians can import appointment notes, labs, medications, and specialist documents in a read-only flow, with some deployments embedding ChatGPT inside Epic charts. OpenAI also added a Healthcare Public Data plugin covering ClinicalTrials.gov, CMS Coverage, RxNorm, DailyMed, and PubMed, and cited 99.1% safe responses across 4,300 physician-validated tests. Access is governed through workspace admins, HIPAA BAAs, RBAC, SSO, and audit logs.

Source

F5 Relevance

EHR-to-LLM connectors are API-heavy, regulated traffic. F5 API gateway, WAF, and identity-aware access can protect Epic FHIR/API surfaces and ChatGPT connectors, with logging suited to HIPAA audit requirements.
