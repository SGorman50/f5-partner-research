NUTANIX July 21, 2026
Nutanix to Demonstrate Enterprise AI Innovations for the Agentic AI Era at AMD Advancing AI 2026
Nutanix will showcase a two-tier enterprise AI strategy at AMD's Advancing AI 2026 event: routing long-lived, complex reasoning tasks to frontier models while sending high-volume tasks to optimized private-infrastructure models. The approach centers on an agent gateway that governs policy, routing, access, and cost, running on Nutanix Cloud Platform with Nutanix Enterprise AI alongside AMD EPYC CPUs and Instinct MI355X GPUs.
F5 Relevance
An agent gateway that governs routing, policy, access, and cost across frontier and private-infrastructure models is directly adjacent to F5's API gateway and AI traffic management capabilities — a natural integration point for policy-driven request routing and access control in agentic AI deployments.
NVIDIA July 20, 2026
NVIDIA NVLink: The Scale-Up Network for AI Factories
NVIDIA detailed its sixth-generation NVLink scale-up fabric for AI factories, delivering 3.6 TB/s per-GPU bandwidth and 260 TB/s rack-level bandwidth in a 72-GPU Vera Rubin NVL72 domain, with GPU-to-GPU latency 3x lower than comparable Ethernet solutions. The architecture adds 130 TFLOPS of in-network FP8 compute for collective operations and introduces NVLink Fusion to connect custom XPUs into NVIDIA's AI infrastructure.
F5 Relevance
As AI factories push scale-up fabrics like NVLink 6 to extreme bandwidth/latency levels, the surrounding scale-out and east-west traffic (inter-rack, inter-cluster, GPU-to-storage) increasingly relies on Ethernet-based fabrics — F5's high-speed networking, load balancing, and AI traffic management portfolio is directly relevant to complementing NVLink-based rack-scale designs at the data-center network layer.
