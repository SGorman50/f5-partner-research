ARISTA July 28, 2026
Arista Patches Actively Exploited VeloCloud Bug as CISA Puts Admins on the Clock
Arista confirmed active exploitation of a critical command-injection flaw (CVE-2026-16812, CVSS 10.0) in its on-prem VeloCloud Orchestrator. The web management interface is exposed by default and requires no credentials to exploit, putting the orchestrator, its managed data, and VeloCloud Edge devices at risk. Patches are available for versions 5.2.3.14, 6.1.3.4, 6.4.2.4, and 7.0.0.1; Arista urges immediate upgrade and restricting web-interface access to trusted networks.
F5 Relevance
A default-exposed, unauthenticated management-plane flaw in a major SD-WAN orchestrator is a timely proof point for F5's WAF/API security value proposition — F5 Distributed Cloud or BIG-IP AWAF can front admin interfaces like VeloCloud Orchestrator to block exploitation attempts even before customers patch, giving F5 field teams a concrete, urgent conversation to have with Arista/VeloCloud-running accounts.

CrowdStrike Joins the Open Secure AI Alliance
On July 27, 2026, CrowdStrike joined the Open Secure AI Alliance (OSAA), a coalition launched by Nvidia together with 30+ technology and security organizations — including Adobe, Cisco, Cloudflare, Databricks, Hugging Face, IBM, Red Hat, Salesforce, SAP, Palo Alto Networks, and Palantir — to develop and share open tools, frameworks, and standards that advance AI safety and security.
F5 Relevance
CrowdStrike joining a 30+ member open AI-security alliance alongside several F5 ecosystem players (Cisco, Red Hat, Databricks, Cloudflare, Palo Alto Networks) signals rapid industry consolidation around shared AI-safety tooling and standards. It strengthens CrowdStrike's positioning as a security layer for AI agents and workloads — an area directly adjacent to F5's AI Gateway / Distributed Cloud app & API security capabilities — and the alliance roster overlaps heavily with F5's existing technology-alliance partners, creating a natural forum to explore joint AI-traffic-security integrations or co-positioning with CrowdStrike Falcon.
DELL July 28, 2026
Simplify Private Cloud: Dell PowerStore and Nutanix Cloud Platform
Dell announced that Dell Private Cloud is now validated with Nutanix Cloud Platform on PowerStore, including automated AHV deployment and Dell hardware support. PowerStore delivers NVMe all-flash performance with transparent Prism integration for Nutanix AHV, requiring no new tools. Available now with Nutanix Cloud Infrastructure (NCI) 7.6, offering full Day 0-Day 2 lifecycle support, non-disruptive upgrades, and single-drive scaling — building on a 14-year Dell-Nutanix partnership.
