Nvidia August 10, 2026

NVIDIA Partners With Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR on AI Compute Financing Platforms Exceeding $500B

On August 10, 2026, NVIDIA announced strategic partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to establish independent AI compute infrastructure financing platforms intended to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital. The MOUs frame NVIDIA compute and full-stack AI infrastructure as an investable asset class, creating dedicated capital pools at scale so frontier labs, enterprises, and AI clouds can finance AI factory buildouts with long-duration, usage-linked economics while supporting NVIDIA ecosystem hardware and software adoption.

F5 Relevance

Accelerated, capital-backed AI factory buildouts multiply demand for secure multi-tenant application delivery, API/gateway controls, encrypted east-west traffic, and AI inference traffic management across GPU clusters—core F5 strengths (BIG-IP, NGINX, Distributed Cloud, AI Guardrails) for partners and customers deploying financed NVIDIA AI factories.
