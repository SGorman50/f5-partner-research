CrowdStrike Q3 ARR Hits $6.26 Billion as Falcon Guardian Crosses $100 Million

CrowdStrike reported fiscal Q3 2027 results on November 23, 2026. Ending ARR reached $6.26 billion, up 27% year over year, with record net new ARR of $419 million. CEO George Kurtz said Falcon Guardian, which secures AI agents, crossed $100 million in ARR in its first year after quarter-end. The same release restated the Zscaler acquisition already covered in an earlier briefing.

Falcon Guardian's early ARR shows enterprises are buying runtime controls for AI agents. F5 can pair API gateway, WAF, and identity-aware access with that agent-security stack where traffic enters the application tier.

Dell Raises AI Server Outlook After a Record Q3

Dell reported fiscal Q3 2027 results on November 24, 2026, with revenue of $54.94 billion and adjusted EPS of $9.45, ahead of expectations. Infrastructure Solutions Group sales were $41.97 billion, including $22 billion of AI-optimized servers, up 291% year over year, and now account for 85% of total revenue. Dell raised its AI-server outlook to about $78 billion and said AI server backlog stands at $132.3 billion. CNBC reported the results.

Source

F5 Relevance

The scale of Dell's AI-server backlog signals sustained demand for high-bandwidth, secure delivery into GPU clusters. F5 can position BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud as the traffic, API, and security layer in front of Dell AI Factory deployments.

HPE Posts Record Q4 as Networking and Cloud & AI Surge

HPE reported fiscal Q4 2026 results on November 23, 2026, with revenue of $14.5 billion, up 50% year over year. Networking revenue was $3.1 billion, up 12.3%, including $397 million of data center networking. Cloud & AI revenue was $11.3 billion, up 68.6%, led by server revenue of $8.8 billion. CEO Antonio Neri said the year made HPE a networking leader and expanded its role in AI infrastructure, and the company raised its fiscal 2027 outlook.

Nutanix Starts a CEO Search as Rajiv Ramaswami Plans to Step Down

On November 24, 2026, Nutanix said its board is working with CEO Rajiv Ramaswami to recruit a successor who will lead the next phase of growth in hybrid multicloud infrastructure and enterprise AI. Ramaswami will stay CEO until the successor is appointed and then serve as a senior advisor. The company did not name a candidate or a transition date.

RedHatNovember 23, 2026

Red Hat and CODESYS Move Industrial Control onto Software-Defined Edge

Red Hat published on November 23, 2026 a collaboration with CODESYS to run a hardware-independent virtual PLC, CODESYS Virtual Control, on Red Hat Device Edge. The stack combines RHEL for edge with lightweight Kubernetes on Arm and x86 industrial PCs, aiming to replace proprietary PLC lock-in and consolidate control, motion, and edge workloads on one platform.

Source

F5 Relevance

Software-defined industrial control on Kubernetes at the edge creates a new ingress, TLS, and API-security surface. F5 NGINX and Distributed Cloud can secure and load-balance those virtual-PLC and edge-application flows.
