AnsibleSeptember 23, 2026

Red Hat Rates Ansible Automation Platform 2.7 Update Important (RHSA-2026:71112)

On September 23, 2026, Red Hat issued RHSA-2026:71112, an Important security and bug-fix update for Ansible Automation Platform 2.7. The advisory covers denial-of-service flaws in python-sqlparse (CVE-2026-54284, CVE-2026-59893, CVE-2026-71491) plus Go stdlib issues in receptor, including TLS KeyUpdate exhaustion (CVE-2026-56862) and XML/ASN.1 recursion DoS. Related critical controller advisories landed the same day for AAP 2.4–2.6.

Source

F5 Relevance. AAP is the execution layer many joint F5 automation playbooks call for certificate, WAF, and BIG-IP changes. Controller and receptor DoS or RCE bugs sit on the same control plane that pushes F5 config, so alliance engineers should flag the 2.7 update in shared runbooks.
CiscoSeptember 23, 2026

Cisco and Omdia: More Than Half of Enterprises Already Run AgenticOps in Production

On September 23, 2026, Cisco released The Impact of Agentic AI on Network Operations with Omdia. The study finds 51% of organizations already run agentic AI in production NetOps, 95% say existing AIOps tools cannot keep up, and 86% want a single integrated platform rather than another point tool. Four in five are comfortable giving agents a high or fully autonomous role, but more than two-thirds require detailed explainability for agent-driven actions.

Source

F5 Relevance. Agentic NetOps that reroutes traffic and closes incidents without a human in the loop is a direct adjacency to F5 application delivery. F5 can position BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud policy, identity, and audit trails as the guardrail layer those autonomous agents must call before changing production traffic.
CrowdStrikeSeptember 23, 2026

BeyondTrust Feeds Privilege Telemetry into CrowdStrike Falcon Next-Gen SIEM

On September 23, 2026, BeyondTrust launched integrations that stream Pathfinder identity and privilege events into CrowdStrike Falcon Next-Gen SIEM. Endpoint Privilege Management policy changes and blocked executions, Password Safe privileged-access events, and Privileged Remote Access session activity can be correlated with Falcon endpoint, identity, and cloud telemetry. The integrations are listed in the CrowdStrike Marketplace.

Source

F5 Relevance. Privilege-elevation and session telemetry landing in Falcon is the same identity context F5 uses for zero-trust access and API authorization. A joint story can tie Falcon identity signals to F5 access policy so privileged sessions to apps and APIs are gated, not only detected after the fact.
EpicSeptember 23, 2026

Epic Says Roadmap Is Unchanged While It Uses Project Glasswing AI Against Its Codebase

On September 23, 2026, Epic told Becker's and In Business Madison that its development roadmap is unchanged after a Modern Healthcare report suggested a cybersecurity pause. Epic said it is participating in Anthropic's Project Glasswing and using Mythos models to search its several-hundred-million-line codebase for vulnerabilities, while still advancing Agent Factory, EpicOps, and interoperability to speed prior authorization. Epic warned the AI security work may generate more urgent fixes.

Source

F5 Relevance. AI-assisted vulnerability hunting on the Epic codebase, plus faster prior-auth APIs, raises the volume of FHIR and MyChart traffic that must stay available during urgent patches. F5 can position WAF, API security, and identity-aware delivery in front of Epic Connect and MyChart during those fix windows.
HPE GreenLakeSeptember 22, 2026

QuEra and HPE Bring Fault-Tolerant Neutral-Atom Quantum Systems onto Cray On-Premises

On September 22, 2026, QuEra Computing and Hewlett Packard Enterprise announced a collaboration to deploy fault-tolerant neutral-atom quantum systems inside customer data centers on the HPE Cray supercomputing platform. The program covers joint testbeds, hybrid algorithm co-design, software interoperability, and system-level benchmarking, with cloud access to QuEra's Libra system planned for 2028. Trade coverage also ties the on-demand path to HPE's GreenLake hybrid quantum-classical services.

Source

F5 Relevance. Hybrid quantum-classical jobs on Cray still move through classical networks, APIs, and identity boundaries that F5 already secures in HPC and GreenLake estates. F5 can extend that story to quantum control-plane and result APIs so on-prem quantum workflows inherit the same TLS, WAF, and zero-trust controls as the rest of the AI factory.
NvidiaSeptember 23, 2026

IonQ Will Install the First QPU at NVIDIA's Accelerated Quantum Research Center

On September 23, 2026, IonQ said its Superion 256 quantum processor will be the first on-premises QPU at NVIDIA's Accelerated Quantum Research Center. The system will link directly to an NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 via NVQLink, with workloads orchestrated by CUDA-Q. Installation at the center and first customer deliveries are scheduled for 2027. This is a confirmed partnership, not an acquisition.

Source

F5 Relevance. NVQLink between a QPU and a GB200 rack is a new high-value east-west path inside the AI factory. F5 can engage NVIDIA and IonQ on securing the classical control and result APIs around that link — TLS, identity, and API protection — without sitting in the quantum interconnect itself.
