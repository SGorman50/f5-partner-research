Entel Digital and Red Hat Partner on a Chilean Sovereign Cloud

G5 Noticias reported on October 30, 2026, and ad-hoc-news.de restated on November 1, that Entel Digital and Red Hat will build a sovereign cloud for Chilean organizations. The stack uses Red Hat OpenShift on Kubernetes so customers can run containers and virtual machines with data residency, local jurisdiction, and governance over models and agents. Entel plans to tie the platform to its network, edge, and CyberSOC, and said sovereignty could later extend toward 5G edge processing. The item is reported by third-party outlets; a Red Hat newsroom confirmation was not found in this run.

Source

F5 Relevance

A sovereign OpenShift cloud that also fronts AI agents and a carrier CyberSOC is a natural fit for F5 in front of the cluster: NGINX Ingress and BIG-IP for Kubernetes ingress, Distributed Cloud for multi-site delivery, and WAF plus identity-aware access on the API paths into models and CyberSOC telemetry.
