RedHat July 8, 2026
Demystify the Architecture of OpenShift Hosted Control Planes
This Red Hat Developer article breaks down the architecture of OpenShift Hosted Control Planes (HCP), covering pre-provisioning decisions around storage performance, network topology, and capacity planning. It details how OpenShift Virtualization (KubeVirt) is used for hosted cluster worker nodes and explains networking options (localnet vs. VLAN-based, NNCP) that affect IP allocation and performance in centrally-managed, resource-efficient cluster deployments.
F5 Relevance
Hosted Control Planes centralize Kubernetes management and introduce new networking topology choices (VLANs, overlays) that directly affect ingress and load-balancing design — relevant territory for F5's NGINX Ingress Controller and BIG-IP Container Ingress Services as OpenShift customers adopt HCP at scale.
