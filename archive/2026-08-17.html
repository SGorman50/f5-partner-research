Nvidia August 14, 2026

Nvidia Discloses $21 Billion SpaceX Stake as Musk Commits Exclusively to Nvidia AI Chips

Nvidia disclosed in an SEC filing that it holds 122.8 million SpaceX Class A shares, valued at about $21 billion at the end of Q2 2026 (about $17.2 billion at Friday's close). The stake originated from Nvidia's $10 billion investment in xAI, which SpaceX later acquired. On SpaceX's Q2 earnings call, Elon Musk said SpaceX will exclusively use Nvidia chips in its AI data centers and expects a significant allocation of Vera Rubin GPUs next year for training and inference of frontier models.

F5 Relevance

An exclusive SpaceX AI-factory build on Nvidia GPUs, plus a Vera Rubin allocation, is another large east-west AI fabric that will need high-speed Ethernet, GPU traffic management, and in-line security. F5 can position BIG-IP, NGINX, Distributed Cloud, and AI Guardrails as the delivery and inspection layer between SpaceX/xAI training clusters, inference endpoints, and the public internet.

Epic August 14, 2026

FTC Examines Epic Systems for Potential Antitrust Violations Around Data Access and Noncompetes

STAT reported on August 14 that the FTC is examining Epic Systems, the largest U.S. EHR vendor, for potential antitrust violations. Investigators have contacted at least four people in or advising health-care businesses that interface with Epic. Sources said the inquiry covers employee noncompete-style agreements and whether Epic uses its market position and hospital leverage to block rival technology vendors from accessing patient data needed to operate.

F5 Relevance

Any forced opening of Epic data interfaces would increase FHIR/API and imaging-exchange traffic that F5 already fronts for health systems. F5 can help hospitals keep API gateways, identity, and WAF controls consistent if more third-party apps are allowed to attach to Epic, while watching for new integration partners if access rules change.
