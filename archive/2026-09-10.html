CiscoSeptember 9, 2026

Cisco Integrates Axis Physical Security Devices into the Meraki Dashboard

On September 9, 2026, Cisco and Axis Communications announced an integration that lets customers deploy, connect, and manage Axis network video devices through the Meraki dashboard inside Cisco Cloud Control, using Axis Cloud Connect. IT and physical-security teams can see Axis cameras alongside Cisco networking gear and Cisco Smart Cameras, with centralized health, firmware, patch, and connectivity visibility. The companies say the integration is privacy-by-design (encryption, secure boot, anonymized telemetry) and that customer data remains customer-owned.

F5 RelevanceUnifying camera/IoT traffic with campus and branch networking expands the east-west and north-south inspection surface F5 already serves with BIG-IP, NGINX, and Distributed Cloud. F5 can partner on API/identity controls for Cloud Control, WAF/DDoS in front of video management apps, and zero-trust access for physical-security operations teams.

Dell AI Data Platform Brings PowerScale for Azure as an Enterprise Corpus Layer

Red Hat AI 3.5 Adds EvalHub, NeMo Guardrails, and Multi-Tenant GPU Controls

On September 9, 2026, Red Hat launched Red Hat AI 3.5 to scale and govern production AI agents. Scality Launches Maestro Fleet Management for ARTESCA Backup-as-a-Service

On September 9, 2026, Scality announced general availability of Scality Maestro 1.1, a fleet-management platform for cloud and managed service providers running many ARTESCA object-storage deployments.
