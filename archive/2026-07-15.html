NVIDIA July 14, 2026
Why Performance per Watt Is the Ultimate Metric for AI Infrastructure Efficiency
NVIDIA argues that performance-per-watt, not raw throughput, is now the defining metric for AI infrastructure efficiency, spotlighting the Blackwell NVL72/GB300 platform and upcoming Vera Rubin systems. Gains are credited to 6th-gen NVLink Switch domains, in-network compute (SHARP), and inference-software techniques such as NVFP4 quantization, disaggregated serving, and KV-cache offloading/routing — citing up to 5x performance-per-watt gains for models like DeepSeek V4 in a month.
