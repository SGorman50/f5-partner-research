DellNovember 16, 2026

Dell Expands the AI Factory with NVIDIA at SC26 and Names F5 in a Validated AI Security Solution

At Supercomputing 2026 on November 16, Dell announced updates to the Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA covering data preparation, agentic infrastructure management, and validated solutions. New Dell AI Data Platform capabilities are meant to turn enterprise data into governed context for AI agents on infrastructure the customer owns. Dell also listed updated validated solutions through the Dell AI Ecosystem Program, including AI Security with F5 and JFrog, an agentic platform with DataRobot, and observability with Dynatrace.

Source

F5 Relevance

Dell naming F5 alongside JFrog in a validated AI Security solution on the Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA is a direct joint-solution opening. F5 should use that listing to push reference architectures for securing agent and inference APIs, TLS, and data-in-flight paths on Dell AI Factory racks.

NvidiaNovember 17, 2026

NVIDIA Board Creates a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee

NVIDIA said on November 17 that its board has established a standing National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson, with directors Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri. The committee will oversee national security policy, export-control compliance, and global trade risk, meet at least quarterly, and retain outside legal and trade experts. NVIDIA’s National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team will report to the committee and to interim CEO Colette Kress.

Source

F5 Relevance

Export-control oversight at the board level, days after the CEO transition, is a signal that NVIDIA’s AI infrastructure shipments and partner stacks will face tighter governance. Alliances that depend on NVIDIA platforms should expect more scrutiny on where systems ship and how models and agents are accessed.

NvidiaNovember 16, 2026

NVIDIA Expands the Vera CPU Rack-Scale Platform for Scientific and Agentic Workloads

On November 16, NVIDIA announced the Vera CPU rack-scale platform for high-performance computing, pairing its first fully custom data center CPU with Quantum-X800 InfiniBand. A rack brings together 192 Vera CPUs and is described as delivering more than 200 TB/s of memory bandwidth, with claimed gains versus x86 on workloads such as OpenFOAM and weather research. Dell Technologies, HPE, Lenovo, and Supermicro are slated to offer the platform beginning in early 2027.

Source

F5 Relevance

Vera racks landing through Dell, HPE, Lenovo, and Supermicro extend the AI and HPC factory pattern F5 already tracks. Those systems will sit behind high-speed fabrics and agentic services that need secure ingress, traffic steering, and API control.

CrowdstrikeNovember 17, 2026

Hitachi Systems India and CrowdStrike Open a Bengaluru Centre for AI Security Delivery

PTI, via Rediff, reported on November 17 that Hitachi Systems India launched an Advanced Global Delivery Centre in Bengaluru with CrowdStrike. The centre will deliver CrowdStrike Falcon Guardian AI detection and response across endpoints, identity, cloud, data, and AI, and will take part in CrowdStrike’s Project QuiltWorks work on frontier AI risk. Hitachi will provide advisory and remediation through CrowdStrike’s FAIRR service for organizations in India, Asia Pacific, and West Asia.

Source

F5 Relevance

A partner-operated delivery centre built around Falcon Guardian and frontier AI risk services is a channel motion F5 should watch. Joint accounts will need inspection and policy on the AI traffic those services protect.

CiscoNovember 16, 2026

Cisco and Red Hat Ansible Move Validated Designs from Manual Guides to Automated Playbooks

Cisco’s November 16 DevNet post describes how Cisco Validated Designs now ship with Red Hat Ansible automation in one repository, so customers adapt a tested data center design instead of deploying a static document. The Intersight Ansible collection is at 137 modules, and Day 2 operations emphasize drift management plus RBAC and audit logs. A previewed AI Pod CVD would automate an inferencing stack spanning UCS X-Series, Nexus, and storage.

Source

F5 Relevance

This is a concrete pattern for how AI infrastructure gets stood up on Cisco networking. F5 can join that validated path as the application and API delivery layer, so load balancing, WAF, and TLS termination are automated alongside Nexus and UCS.

On November 16, Cisco published a DevNet update on a multi-year collaboration that pairs Cisco Validated Designs with Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform.
