Kuwait’s Wren House Buys GIC’s 80% Stake in Equinix xScale

Data Center Dynamics reported on November 27, 2026 that Wren House Infrastructure Management, the infrastructure arm of the Kuwait Investment Authority, is acquiring GIC’s 80% stake in the European xScale joint venture with Equinix. Equinix keeps a 20% share and will still manage and operate the sites in Dublin, Frankfurt, London, and Paris. Close is expected in Q1 2027. Terms were not disclosed.

Source

F5 Relevance

A new majority owner of Equinix’s European hyperscale JV does not change who runs the fabric, but it can reset investment pace for AI and cloud capacity in four core metros. F5 should keep Equinix interconnection and Fabric conversations tied to secure multicloud AI delivery as that capital plan settles.

CBS-backed Report Ties Some U.S. Nvidia Partners to China-linked Firms

SiliconANGLE reported on November 26, 2026 on a CBS News investigation, conducted with C4ADS, into U.S.-based Nvidia partners. It says preferred partner RiCloud AI is a unit of Shenzhen-based Yuxing, that Topmost is linked to a businessman indicted over alleged GPU smuggling, and that Inspur subsidiary Aivres has exported about $3 billion in Nvidia-powered servers to Southeast Asia since 2024. Inspur is on the U.S. Entity List.

Source

F5 Relevance

Export-control risk around Nvidia partners can stall AI factory builds that F5 is asked to secure. F5 should track which GPU supply paths customers actually use so networking and security designs are not tied to a partner that may lose access to hardware.

IntelNovember 27, 2026

Intel Patent Describes AI Inference Inside Network Switches

DailySynapse reported on November 27, 2026 on Intel patent application US 2026/0312885 A1 for switches that run registered AI models on local inference hardware. A tenant registers a model, service ID, and SLA. The switch loads the model, schedules requests, and returns results without a trip to a distant data center. Cited uses include video threat detection and speech.

Source

F5 Relevance

Inference moving into the switch is a direct overlap with F5’s AI traffic and security role. If models execute on the network path, F5 can argue for policy, identity, and inspection in front of those in-switch services rather than treating the switch as a dumb forwarder.

NetAppNovember 25, 2026

NetApp Trident 26.10.1 Fixes In-Use Volume Detach and Documents StorageGRID COSI

NetApp’s Trident release notes, updated November 25, 2026, warn not to upgrade to 26.10.0 because node pods can force-detach volumes still in use. Trident 26.10.1 fixes that Kubernetes startup bug. The same notes add COSI-based dynamic provisioning of object buckets from StorageGRID, plus NVMe data-LIF refresh and ONTAP volume autosize for SAN drivers.

Source

F5 Relevance

COSI bucket provisioning from StorageGRID puts S3 object flows under Kubernetes control, which is exactly where F5 secures and steers data in flight. The detach bug is also a reminder that storage upgrades need the same careful traffic drain F5 already does for app cutovers.

Cisco Partner Study Finds AI Buyers Stuck on Cost, ROI, and Security

Cisco’s 2026 Global AI Partner Study, covered November 25, surveyed 1,702 partners in 30 countries. 92% say customers have raised AI running-cost concerns and only 43% say ROI is meeting expectations, while high awareness of AI-specific security threats among partners fell to 29% from 35%. Source
