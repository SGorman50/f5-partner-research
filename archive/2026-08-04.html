CrowdStrike Fal.Con 2026 Sells Out at Record Pace as the Industry Races to Secure AI

On August 3, 2026, CrowdStrike announced that Fal.Con 2026 sold out faster than any prior year. More than 10,000 attendees, 4,000 organizations from 71 countries, and 150 ecosystem sponsors will convene at Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas (August 31–September 3, 2026) under the theme “Securing the AI Revolution.” The agenda includes a George Kurtz keynote with AI-era architects, 500+ sessions (200+ customer- and partner-led, including AWS, Anthropic, Dell, Google, NVIDIA, and OpenAI), a new Trust in AI CxO Exchange for CIOs/CISOs on AI-driven threats and governance, a Day Zero Threat Research Summit, CrowdStrike University training, 100+ hands-on workshops, and the Global Partner Summit, plus Fal.Con Digital on-demand access.
