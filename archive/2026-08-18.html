Nvidia August 17, 2026

Nvidia Backs Up to $105 Billion in Financing for OpenAI Ohio AI Data Center

On August 17, 2026, Nvidia disclosed it will provide up to $105 billion in credit and compute for an OpenAI AI data center at SB Energy's PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio. The facility will support an initial 4.25 GW of IT load with an option for another 3.75 GW, with capacity coming online in phases starting in 2028. Nvidia will be the exclusive compute supplier on its DSX AI factory platform and will also invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy. CEO Jensen Huang framed the deal as securing long-lived infrastructure that can be upgraded with each new GPU generation.

F5 Relevance

A multi-gigawatt OpenAI factory exclusively on Nvidia DSX will generate massive east-west GPU fabric, north-south inference, and S3/object-storage training traffic that needs application delivery, API security, and TLS offload at scale. F5 can position Distributed Cloud, BIG-IP, and NGINX as the secure front door and multi-tenant traffic layer for this class of AI factory buildout.

Nvidia August 18, 2026

LG Electronics and Nvidia Agree to Train Robots on 100,000 Hours of Data

On August 18, 2026, LG Electronics CEO Ryu Jae-cheol met Nvidia senior director of Omniverse and Robotics Madison Huang at LG's Yangjae R&D campus in Seoul. The companies agreed to combine LG's DataFactory robot bootcamp with Nvidia physical AI technology to train on roughly 100,000 hours of robot data — about 12 years' worth — within the year. The meeting follows last week's discussion between LG Chairman Koo Kwang-mo and Jensen Huang and deepens the pair's physical AI collaboration.

F5 Relevance

Physical-AI data factories move large volumes of unstructured sensor and video data into GPU training clusters — the same S3-to-GPU and inference-serving paths F5 already targets. Partnership conversations around securing and load-balancing those data pipelines, plus identity-aware access to Omniverse and robotics APIs, are a natural F5 angle.

Epic August 17, 2026

Epic Launches Coverage Requirements Discovery API for Real-Time Prior Authorization

Published August 17, 2026, Epic announced that Ochsner Health, Froedtert ThedaCare, Denver Health, and Summit Health are live with its Coverage Requirements Discovery (CRD) API. The API automatically checks prior-authorization requirements for UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, and Network Health inside the EHR at order or scheduling time, using HL7 FHIR-based APIs required by a federal interoperability rule due January 1, 2027. Sixteen additional payers are testing the interface, and the four health systems went live five months ahead of the CMS deadline.

F5 Relevance

CRD is a high-volume, PHI-bearing FHIR API between hospitals and payers. F5 API gateway, WAF, mTLS, and identity-aware access can sit in front of these healthcare APIs to enforce consistent auth, rate limits, and inspection as Epic expands real-time payer connectivity.
