AnsibleSeptember 11, 2026

Red Hat Closes the AIOps Loop Between Splunk Observability Cloud and Ansible Automation Platform

On September 11, 2026, Red Hat published a technical blog describing a production AIOps pipeline that turns Splunk Observability Cloud alerts into governed Ansible remediations. Splunk OpenTelemetry Collectors feed metrics, traces, and logs into Splunk Observability Cloud; webhooks or the Splunk add-on for Event-Driven Ansible then match events to rulebooks that restart services, scale deployments, or rotate credentials under RBAC and audit trails. The cisco.splunk_otel_collector Ansible Content Collection is positioned as the fleet-scale way to deploy and update collectors across Linux, Windows, and Kubernetes.

Source

F5 Relevance

Event-driven Ansible remediations that restart services, rotate certificates, and scale Kubernetes workloads sit next to F5 BIG-IP, NGINX Ingress, and Distributed Cloud policy. F5 can contribute playbooks that drain pools, rotate TLS, or apply WAF and API security changes as the trusted execution step after Splunk detectors fire.

NvidiaSeptember 11, 2026

Nvidia Reported in Talks to Anchor Anthropic's Mega IPO With Up to $10 Billion

Reuters reported on September 11, 2026 that Anthropic is in talks to bring Nvidia in as an anchor investor in what could be the largest IPO on record, with Anthropic seeking to raise as much as $100 billion at around a $2 trillion valuation. One source said Nvidia is considering investing up to $10 billion; the plans remain under discussion and could change. The reported talks would deepen already-complex compute ties: Anthropic is a major Nvidia GPU customer while also diversifying onto AWS Trainium, Google TPUs, and in-house custom silicon. Anthropic declined to comment; Nvidia did not immediately respond.

Source (Reuters via Euronext)

F5 Relevance

An Nvidia-anchored Anthropic listing would further concentrate frontier-model traffic on Nvidia-powered clouds and custom clusters. F5 AI traffic management, API gateway, and TLS/identity controls are the delivery and security plane for Claude-class inference endpoints, KV-cache routing, and multi-cloud failover as Anthropic scales compute across Azure/Nvidia, AWS, and Google.

Red HatSeptember 11, 2026

LTM Partners with IBM and Red Hat on Lightwell AI-Driven Open-Source Remediation

On September 11, 2026, CIO First reported that global services firm LTM is collaborating with IBM and Red Hat on Lightwell, an AI-powered trust infrastructure that automates vulnerability remediation in open-source software supply chains. The pitch is to move enterprises beyond SCA detection into production-safe, dependency-aware patching on Red Hat OpenShift and IBM hybrid cloud, addressing the gap created as generative AI accelerates both vulnerability discovery and code generation. LTM will provide engineering and DevSecOps services under its IBM Platinum Partnership to operationalize the framework at customer scale.

Source

F5 Relevance

Automated OSS remediation on OpenShift still has to land safely on ingress, WAF, and API gateways. F5 NGINX Ingress Controller and BIG-IP can be the controlled change window for patched images and can inspect north-south and east-west traffic while Lightwell-driven rebuilds roll through Kubernetes clusters.
