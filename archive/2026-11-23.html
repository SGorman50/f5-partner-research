CROWDSTRIKE
November 23, 2026

CrowdStrike Agrees to Acquire Zscaler for About $43.9 Billion

On November 23, 2026, CrowdStrike and Zscaler announced a definitive all-stock agreement under which CrowdStrike will acquire Zscaler at about $43.9 billion. Zscaler shareholders will receive 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per share. The companies say the combination brings Zscaler’s Zero Trust network security onto the Falcon platform, expands CrowdStrike’s addressable market by more than $185 billion, and is framed as Zero Trust for AI Safety on agentic traffic. Close is expected in the second half of CrowdStrike’s fiscal 2028, subject to approvals. Zscaler CEO Jay Chaudhry is expected to keep leading the Zscaler business and join CrowdStrike’s board.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

A combined endpoint and Zero Trust exchange will sit on the same application and agent traffic paths F5 secures with BIG-IP, Distributed Cloud, and WAF. F5 should map where Falcon and Zscaler inspection overlaps or complements F5 delivery, especially for AI-agent last-mile access.

DELL
November 21, 2026

Firebird Selects Dell for the Next Phase of Armenia’s AI Cluster

On November 21, 2026, neocloud Firebird said it selected Dell Technologies for the next phase of its AI infrastructure expansion in Armenia. The first phase in Hrazdan already runs on Dell PowerRack with PowerEdge servers and NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs. The next phase is meant to add training and inference capacity for global enterprise customers while keeping a sovereign environment.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Sovereign AI factories on Dell and NVIDIA Blackwell create GPU east-west traffic and public inference APIs that need load balancing, TLS, and WAF. F5 can attach to Dell AI Factory designs as the secure delivery layer for multi-tenant neocloud inference.

NETAPP
November 20, 2026

AWS Launches Third-Generation FSx for NetApp ONTAP

On November 20, 2026, AWS launched a third generation of Amazon FSx for NetApp ONTAP with up to 216 GB/s throughput and 8.6 million SSD IOPS per file system, about 3x throughput and 3.6x SSD IOPS versus the prior generation. Each HA pair now offers up to 18 GB/s and 720,000 SSD IOPS. It is available in US East (N. Virginia and Ohio) and US West (Oregon).

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

Higher-throughput ONTAP in AWS raises the bar for securing NFS, SMB, and related data paths into AI and design workloads. F5 Distributed Cloud and BIG-IP can sit in front of those file services for identity-aware access, TLS, and multi-cloud traffic steering.

Nutanix and Raya IT Sign a Cloud Cooperation MoU in Egypt

On November 22, 2026, Nutanix and Raya Information Technology signed a strategic memorandum of understanding at Cairo ICT 2026. The MoU sets a framework to explore cloud services for government, enterprise, and commercial customers in Egypt, with Raya folding Nutanix into its integration and data-center offerings and Nutanix providing training and certifications.

Source

F5 RELEVANCE

A regional integrator putting Nutanix hybrid cloud into government and enterprise accounts is a joint-solution opening for F5 application delivery and WAF on Nutanix AHV and NKP, especially where sovereign-cloud buyers want local traffic security.
