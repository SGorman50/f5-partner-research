RED HAT June 25, 2026

Implement mTLS and Zero Trust with cert-manager and trust-manager on OpenShift

Red Hat Developer published a June 25, 2026 article detailing a new technology preview of trust-manager integrated with the cert-manager operator for OpenShift. Trust-manager automates distribution and live updating of custom CA bundles across Kubernetes workloads, enabling scalable zero-trust and mTLS deployments. The combination supports automated TLS certificate lifecycle management and broader trust management for OpenShift clusters, reducing manual effort and enabling faster CA certificate rotations.

F5 Relevance

This directly intersects with F5’s certificate lifecycle management (CLM), ACMEv2, and mTLS capabilities. OpenShift customers automating CA bundle distribution via trust-manager create an integration opportunity for F5’s NGINX Ingress Controller and BIG-IP to act as the TLS termination layer that consumes and enforces these certificates — a joint reference architecture story for zero-trust on Kubernetes.
