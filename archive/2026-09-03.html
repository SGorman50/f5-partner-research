Arista Warns Customers of AI-Driven Surge in EOS and VeloCloud Security Advisories

Network World reported on September 2, 2026 that Arista is pre-announcing an elevated batch of security advisories for EOS and VeloCloud, with full CVE details scheduled for September 9. CTO Kenneth Duda and CISO Jason Bevis said AI-enabled vulnerability discovery — using models such as Mythos and Daybreak and early access to Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and Project Glasswing — is finding more flaws in Arista software at machine speed. The company expects higher advisory volume for at least the next few months and is asking customers to subscribe to advisories and use CloudVision’s Compliance Dashboard to triage.

September 2, 2026

Cisco Patches Critical Nexus 9000 Silicon One Remote Code Execution Flaw (CVE-2026-20212)

Cisco PSIRT published a Critical (CVSS 9.8) advisory on September 2, 2026 for a Silicon One integration vulnerability on Nexus 9000 Series switches. An unauthenticated remote attacker can reach TCP ports 43210/43211 in the default L3 VRF and execute code with root privileges, or crash the S1HAL process and reload the device. Cisco released software updates and workarounds, including a Live Protect shield for NX-OS; no known public exploits were reported at publication.

Source

F5 Relevance

Nexus 9000 Silicon One switches are the front-end fabric in many Cisco Secure AI Factory and GPU-cluster designs that F5 also serves with ADC, WAF, and API gateway functions. A root-level RCE on the AI interconnect is a high-priority customer conversation for compensating controls, segmentation, and verified patch windows across the F5/Cisco stack.

CrowdStrike and OpenAI Expand Partnership to Secure Codex Agents and Bring GPT-5.6 Cyber to Falcon

On September 2, 2026 at Fal.Con, CrowdStrike announced an expanded partnership with OpenAI to secure Codex agents at runtime with Falcon Guardian (AIDR) and to harness GPT-5.6 Cyber on the Falcon platform. CPP Investments and Equinix Complete $4B atNorth Acquisition for Nordic AI Data Centers

On September 2, 2026, CPP Investments and Equinix completed the US$4 billion acquisition of Nordic data-center operator atNorth. Post-close ownership is about 51% CPP Investments (US$1.3B), 34% Equinix (US$895M), and 10% Partners Group (US$260M), with remaining equity rolled by atNorth stakeholders. atNorth keeps its brand across eight operational sites in five Nordic countries plus a development pipeline aimed at AI, cloud, and HPC; Equinix said the deal is immediately accretive to AFFO per share.

Source

F5 Relevance

Closing a hyperscale/AI colocation platform in the Nordics expands the interconnection footprint where F5 Distributed Cloud, NGINX, and BIG-IP are already used for multi-cloud app delivery. F5 can pursue Equinix/atNorth joint plays for sovereign AI factories: secure inference ingress, TLS/PKI, and API security at Nordic interconnection hubs.
