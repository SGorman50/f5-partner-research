CiscoOctober 9, 2026

Cisco Extends Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA to Vera Rubin Rack-Scale Systems

On October 9, 2026, theCUBE Research reported that Cisco is expanding Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA to rack-scale systems on the Vera Rubin platform. The design pairs Cisco Silicon One on the front end with NVIDIA Spectrum-X on the back end under a shared Nexus One operating model and an NVIDIA Cloud Partner reference architecture. Cisco also made Cisco Cloud Control generally available and extended distributed security with Hypershield on smart switches.

theCUBE Research

F5 Relevance

A validated Cisco-NVIDIA rack-scale AI factory with Hypershield on smart switches is a joint-solution surface for F5: BIG-IP and Distributed Cloud can sit in front of the same GPU racks for WAF, DDoS, and API policy, while NGINX can front inference endpoints the Nexus One fabric delivers.

CrowdStrike Joins Anthropic Critical Infrastructure Defense Program

CrowdStrike is a founding partner in Anthropic's Critical Infrastructure Defense Program, announced October 8, 2026, with follow-on coverage on October 9. The program pairs Claude models, on-site engineers, and threat research with operators of power, water, transport, and government OT. CrowdStrike is bringing Falcon, including extended IoT visibility, and will use Claude inside Charlotte AI.

CrowdStrike

F5 Relevance

OT and critical-infrastructure AI defense creates a joint story for F5 and CrowdStrike: F5 can terminate and inspect API and OT-adjacent application traffic while Falcon supplies identity and endpoint context. Charlotte AI calling Claude also raises token-routing and guardrail demand that F5 AI traffic management and NGINX can sit in front of.

Epic's AI Security Test Found MyChart Access That Left No Audit Trail

Coverage on October 8-9, 2026 reports that Epic used Anthropic's unreleased Claude Mythos model to find MyChart configurations that could let someone view patient records without an audit-log entry. Epic paused new product work for about six weeks so engineers could patch. MyChart fronts more than 320 million patient records, which Epic says are held by providers, not by Epic.

PYMNTS

F5 Relevance

Unaudited MyChart access is an identity and API-gateway problem. F5 can offer healthcare customers BIG-IP APM and NGINX with OAuth/OIDC and full request logging in front of Epic-integrated portals so access is enforced and recorded even if the application audit trail misses it.
